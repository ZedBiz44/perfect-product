## Offer and assumptions assessed

**Jack’s recorded clarification:** Jack said the holding company could start with IM (internet-marketing) products and expand to unique marketing items: an event-planner template product, someone speaking to chambers, or a one-off WordPress plugin.

**Assessed scenario:** Acquire or revenue-share with an existing small internet-marketing product, then consider adjacent distinctive marketing assets. For one comparable score, use an existing event-planner template product sold to event planners as the first illustrative deal. Keep chamber speaking and WordPress plugins as separate candidate types with different labour and maintenance needs. No target, purchase price, retail price or contract is selected.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand for the assessed template product; Event Excitement may apply to chamber speaking.

Operator allocation, scope and economics below are labelled assumptions. Jack has clarified the direction, not signed supplier, customer or acquisition terms.

## Result and sensitivities

- **Coverage:** 10/10.
- **Concept-fit total /20:** 13
- **Strong fits:** 3/10.
- **Zero conflicts:** None in the explicitly assessed scenario.
- **Shortlist status:** No shortlist signal. A complete total is not proof or launch approval.
- **Revision from WC1:** Bar 2 changes U to 1 under a bounded reusable-template and disciplined-deal assumption, not invented purchase terms. Bar 1 changes 2 to 1 because Jack’s one-off product examples do not justify the former assumed annual licence.
- **Commercial details still to settle:** Check actual target rights, customer access, seller dependence, deal economics and support. Template, speaking and plugin businesses need separate target-level scores before acquisition; no generic portfolio score establishes a good deal.
- **Interpretation:** The model is assessable under stated assumptions. Contract validation is later work; no contracted margin, saving, purchase or delivery result is claimed.
- **Sensitivity:** More per-customer labour weakens bars 2/7/10; reliance on Jack or an irreplaceable seller weakens bars 3/4. Distribution remains conditional on partner acceptance.

The 13/20 total applies to the event-planner template acquisition scenario. Chamber speaking may require recurring personal performance; a WordPress plugin has compatibility and maintenance obligations. Neither inherits this score automatically.
