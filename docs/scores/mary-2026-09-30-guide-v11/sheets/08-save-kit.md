## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies with retainer clients who churn on 'what am I paying for?'
- Offer: A weapon fired at the churn moment: automated proof-of-work packet, white-labeled, plus save/lose tracking that builds a dataset moat.
- Delivery: Digital; triggered assembly from the agency's GHL/data; agency fires it.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): ~$49-149/mo or ~$199-499 one-time + updates (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly subscription with insurance logic (churn moments recur); or one-time + update purchases.
- Founder's role: Not in the path: assembly automated, agency triggers; operators maintain the system; Jack curates at asset level.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-integrate: per-agency data integration (GHL/data connections) is feasible with bounded setup effort.
- A-trigger: the 'what am I paying for?' moment is as acute and frequent as described.
- A-price: unvalidated. Jack's recorded objection ('sold agencies their own job') is preserved as a buying objection, not a kill of this concept.
