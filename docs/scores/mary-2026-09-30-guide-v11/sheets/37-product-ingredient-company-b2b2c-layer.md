## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Upstream businesses who lose money when end users don't get results.
- Offer: A white-label stickiness/implementation layer for someone else's product.
- Delivery: Licensed digital layer; licensee integrates.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): White-label licences ~$197-997/mo or per-seat (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly licences while the host product runs; renewal logic = ongoing stickiness.
- Founder's role: Architect of ingredient designs at asset level; operators maintain; never in per-client path.
- Primary satisfaction shape: -- (structural; end-user shape varies)

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-proof: 'makes their product stickier' must be provable per licensee (assumed demonstrable).
- A-integrate: per-licensee integration is bounded.
- A-price: unvalidated.
