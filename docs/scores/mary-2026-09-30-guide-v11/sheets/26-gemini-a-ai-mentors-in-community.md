- Buyer/payer: Community owners who want hosting relief (B2B licence); end members receive mentoring.
- Offer: Specialized AI agents as localized mentors so the room's culture runs without founder hosting.
- Delivery: Digital persona deployment into the owner's community.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Membership ~$29-99/mo or B2B licence (unvalidated).

- Founder's role: Not in the path in the B2B variant: Jack sells the system; the owner's room runs on AI mentors.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Belonging without hosting
