## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Course creators, coaches, SaaS, agencies whose clients don't implement.
- Offer: Behavior-change support packs that make clients actually do the thing.
- Delivery: Digital packs bolted onto the host's course/retainer/software; white-label.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Licence ~$97-497/mo white-label, or $29-99 end-user add-on (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly white-label licence while the host product runs.
- Founder's role: Curator of pack designs at asset level; operators maintain; never in per-client path.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand
