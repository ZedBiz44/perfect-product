## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Small-to-mid paid Skool/membership/community owners; local-business coaching groups; large free FB AI group owners (secondary).
- Offer: One fresh, useful weekly unit the owner shares under their own name (concept + engagement posts incl. comment-to-get + supplementary pack); niche editions.
- Delivery: Digital weekly drop; Jack/AI produce, owner posts.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $27-49/mo founding hypothesis (unapproved; willingness to pay unproven).
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly subscription; renewal logic = the owner's weekly room-feeding obligation recurs, and each drop removes it.
- Founder's role: Curator/architect of the weekly unit at asset level (taste = moat); production pipeline run by operators + AI; never in per-client path.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-renew: owners renew while drops keep producing member actions; content-subscription churn is the known risk.
- A-price: $27-49/mo is a working hypothesis, not verified willingness-to-pay.
- A-channel: no owned route into paid Skool owners documented; plausible routes (owner communities, Jack's own groups as seed, agency partners) assumed with access requirements unstated.
- A-exec: competent execution with available AI/tools; steady state after reasonable setup.
