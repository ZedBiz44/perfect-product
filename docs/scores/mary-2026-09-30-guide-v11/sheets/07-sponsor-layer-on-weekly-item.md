## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Tool vendors / suppliers as payers; audience owners as distributors of free-to-them value.
- Offer: Sponsor funds distribution of an already-useful weekly unit; agency/owner keeps the client relationship; voluntary next step only.
- Delivery: Funding layer attached to a weekly unit (e.g. Shovel).
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Sponsor ~$500-5,000/mo (wild guess); owners free.
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly sponsor payments; renewal tied to the sponsor seeing audience value.
- Founder's role: Sponsor relationships need human ownership; unit production stays systematized; Jack not in per-client path.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-proof: 'prove owner use first' -- sponsors buy only after demonstrated owner usage.
- A-price: $500-5,000/mo is a wild guess, not a verified price.
