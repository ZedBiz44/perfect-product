## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Group owners who want peer energy without inventing it.
- Offer: An operating system for peer energy: prompts, scoreboard, missions -- the owner owns the community.
- Delivery: Digital licence; owner's staff/VA runs the room.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): ~$49-199/mo seat licence per community (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Monthly seat licence while the community runs; renewal logic = the OS keeps producing peer energy.
- Founder's role: Sells the system; not the mayor; operators maintain the OS; never in per-client path.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Belonging without hosting
