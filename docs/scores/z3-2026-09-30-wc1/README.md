## Interpretation

The updated catalog is better for scoring because it separates the original idea from a labelled working scenario instead of forcing reviewers to invent the missing operating model. Scores here judge the working scenarios, not proven demand.

The strongest recurring pattern is not simply "AI product." High scores come from **reusable production + a natural recurring job + channel leverage + a concrete satisfaction moment**. The weaker concepts usually lose points because they require per-customer human coordination, depend on Jack's ongoing presence, or rely on a buying trigger that remains weak.
