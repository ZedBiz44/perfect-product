## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Tool vendors / suppliers as payers; audience owners as distributors; members as end users.
- Offer: Funding layer: a sponsor pays ~$1,000/month for a defined niche placement in an existing weekly item; the item stays useful without the logo; voluntary next step only.
- Delivery: Placement in the weekly item; partnerships operator handles terms; hosts distribute.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $1,000/month illustrative sponsor price (wild guess; unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Sponsors renew for continued qualified attention, but budget cycles and campaign results make recurrence conditional rather than automatic.
- Founder's role: May negotiate key accounts; 1-3 hrs/week, more during setup.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Harvest Gala (supporting: WC1 working interpretation (sponsor's harvest); original field: Six Pack of Beer Desire for end users)

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-aud: a plausible relevant audience exists to sell (proposed, not sold).
- A-proof: the first sponsor needs proof of audience fit.
- A-price: $1,000/month illustrative, unvalidated.
