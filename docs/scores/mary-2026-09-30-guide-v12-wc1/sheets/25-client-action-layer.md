## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Course creators (illustrative: one repeatable marketing course); coaches, SaaS and agencies as adjacent buyers.
- Offer: White-label action layer on a course: implementation/action packs that close the gap between buying and using; $197/month licence; no individual accountability coaching.
- Delivery: Digital layer; creator supplies outline, reviews the task map, publishes; operator manages mappings and support.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $197/month white-label licence (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each cohort needs implementation prompts and updated tasks, giving the creator a recurring licence reason tied to course delivery.
- Founder's role: Occasional product input; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-map: course mapping is feasible with bounded effort per course.
- A-use: the layer improves implementation beyond worksheets and reminders.
- A-price: $197/month unvalidated.
