## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Local businesses / operators with an immediate bottleneck (illustrative $19 DIY fallback); qualified prospects routed to consulting.
- Offer: Free diagnostic funnel with $19 DIY fallback; the commercial path leads to bespoke consulting for qualified prospects.
- Delivery: Digital assessment; AI summarizes responses and suggests routes; consulting is human per client.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Free gate; $19 DIY; consulting priced separately (unvalidated).

- Founder's role: Qualified prospects are routed into Jack-led advisory work -- directly conflicting with the no-client-work core constraint.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand
