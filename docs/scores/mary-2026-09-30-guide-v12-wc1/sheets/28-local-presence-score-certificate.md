## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies and chambers as payers; end SMBs display the trophy.
- Offer: Scored local-business marketing certificate: published measurable criteria, earned (not invented) accreditation, annual recertification; agency/chamber administers.
- Delivery: Assessment service; AI collects public inputs and proposes scores; a human verifies contested or material facts per business.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $99 per annual assessment (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Annual recertification and multiple client sites provide specific repeat-payment occasions as profiles and criteria change.
- Founder's role: Occasional product standards; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Trophy

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-crit: published measurable criteria exist and are credible.
- A-earn: the certificate is earned, not invented accreditation.
- A-price: $99 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
WC1 is honest about the human cost in this model: per-business evidence verification and appeals are material, which moves B2 and B7 down together. What remains strong is the structure -- annual recertification (B1=2), an ownable Trophy shape (B5=2), and a data-improves-with-scale story (B10=2). The open question is credibility, which only publishing the standard can answer.
