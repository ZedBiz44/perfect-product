## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Operators wanting bounded expert access without a community treadmill.
- Offer: A bounded Q&A community: one scheduled Q&A window per week (1-2 hrs), no daily treadmill; AI triages repeats; archive of answered questions.
- Delivery: Weekly Q&A window; operator triages; archive compounds.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Illustrative membership (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Members pay for continuing bounded access to answers, providing a recurring membership mechanism if the window stays useful.
- Founder's role: Jack's bounded presence is the product's core promise (1-2 hrs/week max) -- archived historical model; not viable under current constraints.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Belonging without hosting
