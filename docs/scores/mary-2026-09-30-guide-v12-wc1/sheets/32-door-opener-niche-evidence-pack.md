## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies entering a new niche (illustrative; also a possible in-house sales tool).
- Offer: One niche evidence pack: before/after examples, objections and answers, niche-specific proof documents -- produced on demand per niche; AI assembles, human checks every claim.
- Delivery: Digital pack; research operator compiles; agency customizes to its market.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $297 illustrative (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each new niche entry creates a new purchase occasion, but a single pack is one-time per niche.
- Founder's role: Occasional standards; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-niche: produced on demand per niche; upfront production is a speculative cost.
- A-check: a human verifies every claim before delivery.
- A-price: $297 unvalidated.
