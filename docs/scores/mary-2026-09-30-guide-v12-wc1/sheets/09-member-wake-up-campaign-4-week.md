## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Paid community owners (local-business / marketers / practical operators).
- Offer: Fixed four-week campaign (Return -> Complete -> Prove -> Continue) to wake quiet members; owner-run, separate from the weekly Shovel format; measurable weekly results.
- Delivery: Digital campaign pack; operator prepares and supports; owner staff run the room.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): US$129 for four weeks, founding pilot (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: A new member cohort or later quiet period creates another campaign occasion; monthly renewal is not inherent in a four-week pack.
- Founder's role: Occasional product oversight; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Harvest Gala (supporting: Six Pack of Beer Desire (original dual shape retained))

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-exec: owners execute the four-week schedule and facilitate responses.
- A-part: uncertain member participation is the known risk.
- A-price: US$129 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
A well-bounded campaign product: the four-week arc gives it a promised harvest moment that the weekly Shovel lacks, but everything (value timing, participation, renewal) depends on the owner executing. WC1 is explicit that US$129 is a considered purchase, which is why B6 moved down from the earlier read.
