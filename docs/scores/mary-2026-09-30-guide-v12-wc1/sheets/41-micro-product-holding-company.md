## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Buyers of small finished template products (per-lane audiences).
- Offer: A holding company for micro-products: a portfolio of small finished template products, each sold to its lane; shared methods across the portfolio.
- Delivery: Digital products per lane; operator maintains; buyers use the template for the defined job.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): Per-product working price (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each product is a stand-alone purchase; the holding earns repeat through additional products bought for additional jobs.
- Founder's role: Sets quality standards; 2-4 hrs/week total (capped).
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand (supporting: the template lane (a finished template product as the example))

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-port: assessed as a product portfolio, not an investment vehicle.
- A-lane: each lane has a real buyer with a defined job.
- A-price: unvalidated.
