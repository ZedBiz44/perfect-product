## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Local-business association as the illustrative payer; broader markets (memberships, coaches, franchises, SaaS CS, chambers) remain open.
- Offer: 'This week is handled': a finished weekly member-value kit -- completed campaign-in-a-box that removes the recurring obligation, not content.
- Delivery: Digital weekly kit; association staff choose and brand assets.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $39/month working price inside the recorded $29-49 founding band (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: The association has a fresh member-value obligation each week, creating a replenishment reason beyond a static library.
- Founder's role: Calibrates practical usefulness once per shared week; 1-3 hrs/week at edition level.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-obl: the payer genuinely feels a weekly member-value obligation.
- A-brand: staff choose and brand without commissioning custom campaigns.
- A-price: $39/month unvalidated.

## Useful improvements (separate note; does not revive a rejected offer)
- Pilot with 3 associations for 6 weeks; measure staff time saved per week -- that number is the B6 argument.

## Unresolved questions
- WTP at $39/month.
- Whether the weekly obligation is felt strongly enough to outsource.
- Association-manager channel access.

## Overall interpretation
The 'operating dependency' framing is what lifts this above a content subscription: the weekly obligation is the recurrence engine (B1=2) and 'this week is handled' is an ownable relief moment (B5=2). The risk is all on the buying side -- whether the obligation is felt and whether staff content plus free AI is 'good enough'.
