## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies and local marketers with dormant client/customer lists.
- Offer: Fixed reactivation campaign pack for a permitted dormant list: messages, workflow pattern and checklist; niche-specific to differ from generic GHL practice.
- Delivery: Digital pack; agency loads its own contacts, checks consent and runs the campaign.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $149 per campaign pack (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Different seasonal offers or niches create repeat occasions, but a buyer can reuse the same messages without repurchasing.
- Founder's role: Occasional exceptions, not campaigns; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Harvest Gala

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-consent: the buyer holds a permitted dormant list.
- A-niche: niche specificity is real enough to differ from existing GHL reactivation workflows.
- A-price: $149 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
Mechanically sound (buyer does all variable work; AI drafts the shared sequence), commercially thin: the recorded objection is that undifferentiated reactivation is already free inside GHL. WC1 keeps that objection, so B6 stays capped and recurrence depends on genuinely distinct seasonal/niche editions.
