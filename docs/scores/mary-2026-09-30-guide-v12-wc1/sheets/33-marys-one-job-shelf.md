## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Owners/operators wanting one finished job per box (per-box audiences, e.g. rural property buyers, marketing agencies).
- Offer: One job per box: a finished operational tool or kit that does one defined task; per-box price ($79-149 one-time, or $29-39/month); each box separate; no cross-promotion, no community.
- Delivery: Digital box; operator maintains; buyers use the tool for the defined job.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $79-149 one-time or $29-39/month working prices (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each box is a stand-alone purchase; the shelf earns repeat through additional boxes bought for additional jobs, not through one subscription.
- Founder's role: Sets quality bar per box; 2-4 hrs/week total (capped; not per buyer).
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-box: each box is a genuine one-job tool, not repackaged content.
- A-noise: no cross-promotion between boxes (WC1 distribution rule).
- A-price: working prices unvalidated.
