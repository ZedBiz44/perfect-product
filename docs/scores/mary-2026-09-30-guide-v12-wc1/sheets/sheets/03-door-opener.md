## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Marketing agencies / GHL-style agency owners who struggle to get clients.
- Offer: One tested, niche-specific free win per pack (illustrative: dental-practice profile improvement) with evidence, checklist and outreach script; agency runs it.
- Delivery: Digital niche pack; agency executes prospect work with its own account access.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $149 working price inside the recorded $99-249 range (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Agencies may buy another niche pack or update, but the same niche's opening move can be reused without another payment.
- Founder's role: Occasional product decisions; 0-2 hrs/week at steady state; Jack does not take sales work.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-ev: the niche win is genuinely tested and transferable across prospects.
- A-price: $149 unvalidated.
- A-exec: agencies execute outreach themselves.
