## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies needing a client-facing distribution point (illustrative: an agency delivering client assets).
- Offer: A storefront-less distribution point: the agency's clients download kits/templates without a public store; access control, delivery, and usage guidance.
- Delivery: Digital delivery service; operator runs the platform; agencies manage their own clients.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $49/month illustrative licence (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Clients need assets repeatedly, giving the agency a continuing reason to keep the distribution point live.
- Founder's role: Occasional platform input; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-use: agencies have a real recurring client-delivery need.
- A-store: no-store is meaningfully simpler than the alternatives.
- A-price: $49/month unvalidated.
