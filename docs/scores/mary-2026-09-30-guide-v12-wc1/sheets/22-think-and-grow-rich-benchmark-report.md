## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agency subscribers as an optional layer (illustrative).
- Offer: Quarterly benchmark report: permissioned comparable campaign outcomes, taxonomy and analysis methods; no invented results.
- Delivery: Digital report; research operator manages collection, analyst checks interpretation.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $79 quarterly (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: New quarterly observations can inform recurring planning, giving buyers a reason to pay again if the data remains relevant.
- Founder's role: Occasional standards decisions; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand (supporting: WC1 working interpretation; original field held the Trophy hypothesis, which WC1 finds weaker (a benchmark alone confers no earned status))

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-perm: permissioned comparable outcomes are obtainable at viable cost.
- A-rel: comparisons stay relevant and trustworthy quarter to quarter.
- A-price: $79 unvalidated.
