## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies serving seasonal home-service businesses; associations as secondary.
- Offer: Seasonal campaign editions sold once per season (illustrative: $399 spring-opening edition for home services), niche-specific; agency marks up to clients.
- Delivery: Digital pack; agency runs the client campaign (list, budget, local facts, operation).
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $399 per season per niche edition, inside the recorded $199-997 band (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Distinct seasonal buying windows recur annually, creating a concrete reason to buy refreshed offers and later-season editions.
- Founder's role: Occasional positioning input; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Harvest Gala

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-niche: the edition is genuinely niche-specific, not generic Cash This Week.
- A-exec: agencies run competent campaigns.
- A-price: $399 unvalidated.
