## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Capable local-business owners, local marketers, agencies.
- Offer: Productized one-job tools from 25 years of practice; illustrative first example: $99 review-request tool kit (request generator, QR/landing template, instructions). Niche packs as ladder.
- Delivery: Digital kit; business staff install and operate it without custom implementation by ZedBiz.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $99 for the illustrative kit inside the recorded $79-249 band (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Additional locations, niche packs and useful upgrades can generate repeat purchases; the basic review-request job does not require a monthly subscription.
- Founder's role: Informs the product specification and exceptions; 0-2 hrs/week at steady state.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-cap: the buyer is capable (has website access, can follow instructions).
- A-dist: the kit differs from 'another GHL snapshot'.
- A-price: $99 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
The family thesis in miniature: one finished job, self-serve, operator-run, with the ladder (niche packs, adjacent tools) as the recurrence story. WC1 keeps the catalog's own risk ('another GHL snapshot') front and center in B6. Mechanically strong; commercially unproven.
