## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Paid community owners (local-business / marketers / practical operators).
- Offer: Weekly mission + comment-word + homework: a shared member-action unit with response pack, per niche cohort; owners run it.
- Delivery: Digital weekly mission pack; operator publishes and handles access.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $29-49/month hypothesis (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each week supplies a new achievable member action, so the unfinished job recurs even after last week's mission is completed.
- Founder's role: Jack calibrates mission quality once weekly; 1-3 hrs across the edition, not per buyer.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-run: owners run missions and handle member interaction; ZedBiz does not coach or chase members.
- A-price: $29-49/month unvalidated.
- A-diff: the unit differentiates from free engagement prompts in practice.

## Overall interpretation
The member-action framing is what makes this the strongest Shovel variant: completion is a concrete, ownable satisfaction moment. WC1 is honest that facilitation is real owner work, which caps B9, and that differentiation from free prompts is the B6 question. No founder-constraint conflict in the described model.
