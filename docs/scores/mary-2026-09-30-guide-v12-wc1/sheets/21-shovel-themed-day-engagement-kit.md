## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Existing Shovel owners who want more engagement scaffolding (add-on to #01).
- Offer: Optional themed-day engagement kit layered on the weekly Shovel: extra posts/assets for themed posting days; not the core product.
- Delivery: Digital bundle; operator delivers; customers handle posting and member replies.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): + $15/month order bump on the Shovel subscription (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Weekly themed slots recur, so the add-on supplies fresh scaffolding alongside the base subscription.
- Founder's role: Weekly selection shared with the core edition; within the same 1-3 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-host: product 01 first wins buyers; the add-on has no standalone market.
- A-noise: extra posts add value rather than noise.
- A-price: +$15/month unvalidated.
