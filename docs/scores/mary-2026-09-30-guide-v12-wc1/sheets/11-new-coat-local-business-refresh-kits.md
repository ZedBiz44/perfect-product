## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Agencies, chambers, franchise HQs as resellers; end local businesses.
- Offer: Fixed 'new coat' refresh package for life moments (illustrative: $249 reopening announcement kit): fixed editable layouts, copy patterns, asset sizes; end business supplies approved photos and details; no custom design creep.
- Delivery: Digital kit; reseller serves its client; GHL delivers assets only in the broader concept.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $249 to the reseller for the illustrative kit (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: New openings, offers and seasonal refreshes create repeat occasions across an agency's clients; each individual business may buy infrequently.
- Founder's role: Occasional direction; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Coat of paint

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-scope: bespoke design and repeated revisions stay outside the package.
- A-facts: resellers verify each client's supplied facts and visuals.
- A-price: $249 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
