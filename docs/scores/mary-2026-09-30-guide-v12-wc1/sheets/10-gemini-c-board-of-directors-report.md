## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Local businesses / operators with a named bottleneck.
- Offer: One multi-AI-synthesis diagnostic report for a submitted operating bottleneck: bounded intake, automated multi-perspective analysis, human-signed report; no consultation or implementation upsell.
- Delivery: Digital report; trained analyst signs off each report under a bounded review standard.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $197 per report (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: A different bottleneck or quarterly review can prompt another report, but one report may solve the immediate need without repeat purchase.
- Founder's role: Rare product exceptions only; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand (supporting: Coat of paint (original dual shape retained))

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-in: buyers supply adequate examples in the structured intake.
- A-qual: synthesis quality beats free AI advice consistently enough to justify $197.
- A-price: $197 unvalidated.

## Overall interpretation
The honest version of an AI-report product: WC1 documents material per-buyer analyst review, which caps B2, B7 and B10 together. The concept is coherent (bounded intake, signed report, no upsell trap), but its economics are analyst-throughput economics, not software economics.
