## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Practitioners who build reusable tools (illustrative: a marketing-ops tool builder); buyers of the finished boxes.
- Offer: A syndicate that turns practitioner tools into finished one-job boxes: contributor submits, factory finishes, revenue share; illustrative $99 contributor kit.
- Delivery: Digital boxes via the shelf; operator runs intake and finishing; contributors build, factory packages.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $99 illustrative contributor kit (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Each finished box is a stand-alone purchase; contributors earn on ongoing box sales, giving them a recurring reason to supply new tools.
- Founder's role: Sets quality standards; 2-4 hrs/week total (capped).
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand (supporting: via the illustrative $99 contributor kit (marketing ops tools as the example lane))

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-sup: contributors supply tools worth finishing.
- A-fin: the factory finishing step adds real product value.
- A-price: $99 kit unvalidated.
