## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Membership or chamber owners.
- Offer: $99 event pack: theme, mission, invitations and run-of-show; staff execute the night; no Jack appearance.
- Delivery: Digital pack; the purchaser's staff host; venue and logistics stay with the purchaser.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $99 per event pack (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: A calendar of events supports repeat purchases and seasonal bundles, but a successful format can also be reused without paying again.
- Founder's role: Occasional product input; 0-2 hrs/week; no event duty.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Event Excitement
