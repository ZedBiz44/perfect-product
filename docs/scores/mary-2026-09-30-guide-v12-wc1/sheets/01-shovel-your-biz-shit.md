## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Paid community owners (Skool/membership/coaching groups).
- Offer: Weekly shared drop: one concept + 2-4 engagement posts + supplementary pack; niche editions; owner posts under own name.
- Delivery: Digital weekly drop; operator schedules and delivers.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $27/month working price (unvalidated; other price hypotheses retained).
- Repeat mechanism: Fresh weekly member activities replace the owner's recurring job of finding something useful to share; renewal depends on continued use.
- Founder's role: Jack selects the weekly idea + reviews one shared edition; 1-3 hrs/week at edition level, not per buyer (curator role).
- Primary satisfaction shape: Six Pack of Beer Desire

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-use: owners post the drop weekly; unused content is a real churn risk.
- A-price: $27/month unvalidated.
- A-rights: contributor rights assignable so the edition library can transfer.

## Overall interpretation
