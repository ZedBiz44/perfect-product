## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Industry organizations, agencies, and SaaS companies that already convene customers.
- Offer: B2B licence: an organization runs a bounded peer circle; the system supplies facilitation kit, room rules, AI summarizer and session scripts; the licensee -- not Jack -- is the social center.
- Delivery: Licence + kit; the licensee runs sessions and culture.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $2,400/year working price inside the recorded $1,800-2,400 band (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Circle facilitation and culture maintenance recur every period, giving the licensee a continuing need for the system licence.
- Founder's role: Curator and standards keeper; 0-2 hrs/week.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Belonging without hosting

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-lic: the licensee is the social center; Jack never hosts.
- A-run: licensees run sessions well enough to retain members.
- A-price: $2,400/year unvalidated.
