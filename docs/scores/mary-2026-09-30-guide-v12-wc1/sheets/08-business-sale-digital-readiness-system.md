## Offer snapshot
- Buyer/payer: Business-broker firms, succession advisers, accountants, lenders.
- Offer: Fixed digital-sale-file licence for repeated seller intake: seller workbook, asset inventory, ops/key-person prompts, broker intake, non-legal transition checklist; no valuation or transaction advice.
- Delivery: Licensed digital system; broker brands the intake, invites the seller, collects inventory and reviews gaps.
- Price hypothesis (working assumption, not verified willingness-to-pay): $997 fixed licence (unvalidated).
- Repeat mechanism: Additional offices, seller-volume licences and substantive updates can prompt another payment; a one-time licence alone has weak repeat economics.
- Founder's role: Occasional commercial perspective; 0-2 hrs/week; Jack does not join transactions.
- Primary satisfaction shape: Tool in the hand

## Material assumptions (labelled)
- A-gap: brokers have a specific gap this fills vs adequate existing checklists.
- A-data: private seller data is not presumed transferable business IP.
- A-price: $997 unvalidated.
