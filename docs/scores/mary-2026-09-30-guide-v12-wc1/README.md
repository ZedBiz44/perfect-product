## Method
- Scored the WC1 / WC1.1 VERSION, not the original pitch: each entry file's 'Working concept for assessment' section defines what was scored.
- WC1 are PROPOSED business designs authorized by Jack (2026-09-30), not customer evidence or launch decisions; reasonable assumptions allowed and labelled; labels alone prove nothing.
- Concept-fit mode (guide v1.2): an unbuilt idea can be scored numerically from documented design + business logic + labelled assumptions. U only where a missing detail materially prevents judgment -- WC1/WC1.1 resolved every prior U, so all 41 assessments are complete (10/10 bars).
- 2 = credible specific mechanism under reasonable assumptions; 1 = partial with a meaningful limitation/trade-off; 0 = the described model conflicts with the bar (missing tests alone are not failure).
- Confidence (H/M/L) recorded separately from the score; low confidence does not force 1 or U.
- Founder constraints applied throughout: Jack never in the per-client delivery path; no trust-gated access to each client's books; no community-host treadmill (max bounded weekly 1-2hr Q&A); buyer must feel a painful expensive problem, be reachable, and have time to buy. Documented conflicts earn 0.
- A rejected model remains rejected: zeros stay on the conflicted bars; a score does not revive a rejected offer; catalog statuses unchanged.
