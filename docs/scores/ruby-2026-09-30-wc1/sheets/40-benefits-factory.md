## Offer and assumptions assessed

**Working scenario:** A relationship-owning business or organization buys a recurring communications package for its audience, customized to its brand and approved facts. Agencies, chambers and associations remain possible payers, not a chosen niche. Swag is supplied through a partner; event planning is a separate bounded package. The prior $297 chamber-discount example is withdrawn; actual pricing is not chosen.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire for recurring communication; Event Excitement for the optional event package (working interpretation; original catalog Shape retained).

All WC1 bullets are working assumptions or inferences, not observed results: operator roles are unassigned, prices are hypotheses, and customer/partner access is not claimed. Founder-hour figures are illustrative workload assumptions.
