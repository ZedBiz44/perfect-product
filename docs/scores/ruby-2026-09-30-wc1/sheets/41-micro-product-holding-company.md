## Offer and assumptions assessed

**Working scenario:** Acquire or revenue-share with an existing small internet-marketing product, then consider adjacent distinctive marketing assets. For one comparable score, use an existing event-planner template product sold to event planners as the first illustrative deal. Keep chamber speaking and WordPress plugins as separate candidate types with different labour and maintenance needs. No target, purchase price, retail price or contract is selected.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand for the assessed template product; Event Excitement may apply to chamber speaking (working interpretation; original catalog Shape retained).

All WC1 bullets are working assumptions or inferences, not observed results: operator roles are unassigned, prices are hypotheses, and customer/partner access is not claimed. Founder-hour figures are illustrative workload assumptions.
