## Scoring stance (calibration)

- I scored the **WC1 scenario as written**, using each entry's ten labelled bullets as the bar-by-bar evidence, plus repo sources and recorded decisions. Where WC1 itself records a limitation ("facilitation creates more effort", "per-client verification remains material", "initial mapping is a real project"), that limitation caps the bar at 1 — the working concept's own honesty is part of the evidence.
- **Bar 6 and bar 8 stay Low confidence everywhere** unless Jack's clarification supplies the buying logic (39 bar 6): prices are hypotheses and channels are proposed, never secured. Most bar 6/8 scores are 1, not 2.
- **Zeros are preserved, never averaged away.** Rejected/withdrawn/killed offers (15–19, 26, 27, 36) keep their documented conflicts even where their mechanics now score numerically under WC1. A scored rejection stays a rejection.
- **Two-sided offers get the payer's satisfaction judged** (23, 31 sponsor layers): both sponsor and owner tasks are assessed; coordination burden caps several bars at 1.
- Where I differ from Cody's WC1 run, it is mostly bars where I read WC1's stated limitations as capping a score (e.g. 10 Gemini C per-buyer analyst review → bar 2/7 at 1; 37 CourseFinish per-course mapping → bar 2/7 at 1) or where I credit a documented design more strongly (e.g. 11/12/13 event-and-kit products with founder explicitly off-stage → bar 4 at 2). Each sheet states its own reason.
