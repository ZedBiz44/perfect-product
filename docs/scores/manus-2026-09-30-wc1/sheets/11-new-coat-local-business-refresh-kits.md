## Assessment frame
- **Buyer and payer:** The assessed payer is an agency reseller buying a $249 reopening kit; its local-business client supplies inputs, receives the refreshed set, and is marked up by the agency.
- **Offer and delivery:** A fixed reopening-announcement kit of editable layouts, copy patterns, and asset variants. The end business supplies approved photos and facts; the reseller or business fills approved fields, checks them, and publishes. Bespoke design and repeat revisions are outside scope.
- **Price hypothesis:** $249 per kit to the agency reseller, which may mark it up to the end local business; this is an unvalidated catalog hypothesis.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** An agency can purchase again when different clients reopen, launch an offer, renovate, or need a seasonal reset; any one local business is expected to buy only infrequently.
- **Founder role:** Jack supplies occasional product direction (assumed 0–2 hours weekly); a creative operator packages and quality-checks shared editions, while resellers handle their client relationships and fact checks.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Coat of paint: a local business publishes a coherent refreshed set, sees the before/after reveal, and can show off the new presentation.
- **Founder fit:** conditional

## Material assumptions
- The fixed editable package is enforced so client-specific design, revisions, and installation do not become custom work.
- Agency resellers have enough client life-moment occasions for repeat purchases and can earn a worthwhile markup at the proposed price.
- A trained creative operator can perform shared-edition QA and Jack's role remains bounded to occasional direction.
- AI-generated copy and asset variants can be reviewed reliably, while resellers can accurately verify their clients' photos and facts.
- Design educators, agency trainers, or chamber service partners will regard the occasion-specific kit and reseller margin as sufficient reason to distribute it.
- Fonts, images, contributor rights, and reseller/customer contact rights are licensed or otherwise transferable where required.

## Sensitivities
- The concept changes materially if the payer is the end local business instead of a multi-client agency reseller, because repeat occasions and resale distribution weaken.
- Economics are sensitive to whether $249 includes only a fixed digital kit or creates expectations of custom design, revisions, setup, or publishing help.
- The C3PO and founder-independence fits weaken if AI drafts need substantial human redesign per edition or per client.
- The channel score depends on partner incentive and access; a named intermediary category is not a secured route.

## Improvements separate from score
- Specify a locked contents list, permitted editable fields, revision policy, and publication responsibility so the offer remains a kit rather than a design project.
- Define the agency resale package and an occasion-specific demonstration that makes the $249-to-markup logic immediately legible.
- Document the operator QA checklist, AI prompt and asset workflow, rights register, and handoff so it can operate without Jack.
- Choose one initial channel-owner type and clarify its margin, onboarding materials, and non-exclusive distribution path before treating the route as strong.

## Unresolved questions
- Which agency type has both a recurring local-business refresh workload and authority to buy a $249 kit without a sales call?
- What exact assets and editable fields constitute the finished reopening kit, and who publishes each component?
- Does the agency's expected markup and client outcome exceed its alternatives of Canva, existing staff, or a freelance designer?
- Can human QA remain per shared edition while preserving quality across varied local-business photos, facts, and brand contexts?
- Who owns and can transfer the reseller relationships, customer-contact permissions, fonts, images, and generated assets?

## Overall interpretation
Under the WC1 fixed-kit scenario, New Coat is a strong product-shape fit: reusable occasion IP, a clear transformational reveal, operator-transferable production, and scale benefits through reseller distribution support eight strong bars. The material weaknesses are commercial rather than mechanical: the proposed agency payer must see a clear advantage over templates or its own designer, and the reseller channel is plausible but unproven. Its founder fit is conditional on enforcing a no-custom-design boundary and keeping client fulfillment with the reseller.

## Scope note
This is an independent concept-fit assessment of the stated WC1/WC1.1 scenario. It is not evidence of demand, customer results, margins, partner access, or a launch decision. Existing status remains unchanged.
