## Assessment frame
- **Buyer and payer:** Host application: the Door Opener agency prospecting workflow. The payer is a marketing agency serving dental practices; its staff use the pack with their own prospects.
- **Offer and delivery:** A self-serve $199 dental-niche digital opening pack: a free-win script/checklist plus honestly labelled, public or permissioned sourced demonstrations. The agency adapts and executes the prospect work; it is not ZedBiz-managed sales work.
- **Price hypothesis:** $199 for one dental-niche pack, within the recorded $149–$299 hypothesis; it must be more useful or credible than free audits, generic AI copy, or freely available case studies.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Optional rather than contractual recurrence: an agency may buy another niche pack or a refreshed evidence edition, while the original pack can remain usable for a long time.
- **Founder role:** A research editor maintains evidence and an operator supports the pack; Jack is assumed to make occasional standards decisions (0–2 hours per week), with agencies doing their own sales work.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand: a ready, niche-relevant demonstration and script should make an agency more capable of opening a concrete prospect conversation. It does not promise that the prospect converts.
- **Founder fit:** conditional

## Material assumptions
- A dental-serving agency will pay $199 for a differentiated, evidence-backed free-win pack despite free audits and generic AI alternatives.
- Useful examples can be sourced, honestly bounded, and either publicly reusable or permissioned without making the shared-pack economics uneconomic.
- AI can find candidate evidence and draft scripts while human evidence and rights checks remain a once-per-shared-edition quality-control task rather than buyer-specific fulfillment.
- Niche agency trainers can obtain placement, demonstrate the pack, and accept a reseller margin without requiring ongoing personal selling by Jack.
- The editor/operator role and the evidence standards can transfer from Jack, and the agency accepts the expected work of adapting the win and selling to its own prospects.

## Sensitivities
- A weak distinction from agencies' existing free audits or generic AI outreach would materially reduce the price-to-benefit case.
- Evidence provenance, permissions, and the cost of checking applicability are central to both margin and credibility.
- The repeat model depends on genuinely useful new niches or refreshes rather than a pack that agencies reuse indefinitely.
- Trainer-led distribution is conditional on access, trust, and a margin that justifies promotion.

## Improvements separate from score
- A sharper concept boundary would specify the evidence provenance, reuse-rights status, and claim limits for every demonstration.
- A defined refresh or cross-niche library structure would make the optional repeat-payment mechanism more concrete without implying a subscription exists.
- A clearer non-audit differentiation and the exact agency action enabled by the free win would reduce explanation burden.
- The trainer channel would need a specified demonstration, purchaser handoff, and incentive model to be more than a proposed route.

## Unresolved questions
- Will dental-focused agencies pay for this instead of using their current audits, free case studies, or generic AI-created outreach?
- What outcome evidence is usable, permissioned, and relevant enough to support credible niche claims at $199?
- Can a shared evidence update be produced with bounded human rights and quality work while retaining worthwhile margin?
- Which niche agency trainers can repeatedly reach the payer and why would they choose to promote this pack?
- Does the prospect-facing free win reliably create a useful conversation without turning the product into custom agency sales fulfillment?

## Overall interpretation
This is a coherent, reusable B2B ingredient for an agency's Door Opener workflow, with conditional strengths in transferable library assets, delegated operation, AI-assisted shared production, and scale reuse. Its concept fit remains limited by optional recurrence, uncertain differentiation and willingness to pay versus free audits, rights-sensitive evidence economics, and an unproven trainer route; it remains an exploration-status scenario rather than commercial proof.
