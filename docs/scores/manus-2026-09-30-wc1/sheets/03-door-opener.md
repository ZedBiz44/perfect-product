## Assessment frame
- **Buyer and payer:** A marketing or GHL-style agency owner pays for a dental prospecting pack; the agency, not Jack or the prospect, performs the prospect outreach and free improvement.
- **Offer and delivery:** A self-serve dental niche pack containing a reusable profile-improvement demonstration, checklist and outreach script. AI drafts shared research, scripts and improvement suggestions; human review validates the shared method; agency staff apply it to individual prospects.
- **Price hypothesis:** $149 one-time for the dental pack, within the recorded ~$99–$249 range; a low monthly refill of new niche wins is an alternative, not an established offer.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** An agency may purchase another niche pack or an update/refill, but the same niche opening move is reusable without another payment, so repeat is optional rather than inherent.
- **Founder role:** Jack is assumed to make occasional product decisions (0–2 hours weekly at steady state); an operator updates and checks shared packs, while agency personnel sell and execute the prospect work.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand: on first use, the agency has a concrete, bounded free-win tool that can demonstrate competence and open a prospect conversation.
- **Founder fit:** conditional

## Material assumptions
- A dental profile-improvement action can be standardized, permission-safe and useful across agency buyers without prospect-specific diagnosis by ZedBiz.
- AI can perform the shared drafting work while one human validation step per pack is sufficient and does not become per-buyer fulfillment.
- At $149, contribution remains worthwhile after pack maintenance, affiliate compensation, customer acquisition and support.
- Agency trainers or niche newsletters will accept an affiliate-style placement that can reach agency owners without continuing founder-led selling.
- Agencies with weak prospect pipelines will perceive a material difference between this pack and the free audits or giveaways they already use.
- A trained operator can maintain and check packs so that Jack's involvement stays limited to product exceptions and decisions.

## Sensitivities
- If the profile improvement cannot be applied without extensive prospect permission or custom diagnosis, bars 2, 5, 7 and 9 weaken.
- If free agency audits are functionally equivalent, the $149 value case and buyer conversion weaken, especially bar 6.
- If affiliate placements are unavailable or require continuing founder sales, bar 8 and the practical independence assumption weaken.
- If pack refreshes require substantial per-niche human evidence work, the shared-margin and AI-core conclusions weaken.

## Improvements separate from score
- A narrowly bounded, permission-safe profile-improvement action with a visible before-and-after output would make the free win more distinct from a generic audit.
- An explicit update or new-niche refill definition and cadence would create a clearer repeat-payment mechanism.
- Channel materials that show the trainer or newsletter's affiliate and audience benefit would make the proposed buyer route more concrete.
- A fixed human validation protocol and a stated boundary for prospect-specific exceptions would protect the no-custom-work model.

## Unresolved questions
- What meaningful result differentiates this offer from the free audits and giveaways agencies already use?
- Would agency owners pay $149 for, and actually deploy, a dental pack rather than make their own version with free AI?
- Which profile action can be completed legally and safely before a prospect is a client, and what permission is required?
- Can agency trainers or niche newsletters provide economically viable, repeatable placement without direct founder selling?
- What evidence standard is needed for a claim that the niche-specific win is tested?

## Overall interpretation
Door Opener is a credible reusable, AI-assisted digital tool scenario with transferable library potential and a clear Tool in the hand experience. Its main concept-fit limits are optional rather than inherent repeat revenue, unproven differentiation from free agency audits, prospect-execution friction, and a proposed but unearned distribution route. The recorded hopper status remains appropriate; this assessment is not evidence of demand or approval to act.
