## Assessment frame
- **Buyer and payer:** A paid community owner who subscribes to the base Shovel product; that same owner/payer takes the outcome-comparison upgrade.
- **Offer and delivery:** One shared weekly Shovel edition plus periodic permissioned outcome-comparison reports, delivered as an in-product upgrade. An operator collects and delivers; AI drafts content and aggregates outcomes; human review checks cohorts and interpretation.
- **Price hypothesis:** The WC1 scenario adds $20/month for comparisons to the linked Shovel WC1 working base of $27/month; report cadence and the resulting total-value proposition remain unvalidated.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Fresh weekly actions replenish the owner's recurring member-value job, while new outcome observations create periodic comparison and planning occasions for the continuing upgrade.
- **Founder role:** Jack calibrates the shared content and interpretation, assumed at 1–3 hours per week; an operator handles collection and delivery, with human judgment also checking comparability.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire — the owner has a ready, useful item to publish this week; periodic feedback is a supporting reinforcement, not the primary shape.
- **Founder fit:** conditional

## Material assumptions
- Paid community owners will subscribe to the base Shovel product and will repeatedly submit permissioned outcomes for the upgrade.
- Outcome definitions can be normalized into relevant peer cohorts without turning each report into bespoke analyst work.
- The required consent, ownership, retention and reuse rights make the outcome records a transferable asset.
- A $20/month comparison layer is small relative to the owner's retention or planning benefit despite free AI, PLR and informal peer alternatives.
- AI aggregation and a shared review process remain bounded as participation and niche diversity grow.
- The host product gains paying, participating owners through a non-founder-dependent route; the upgrade checkout alone does not acquire those owners.

## Sensitivities
- Without consistent, permissioned outcome inputs, the analytical asset, report relevance and scale mechanism materially weaken together.
- If each niche requires bespoke cohort definitions or analyst interpretation, reuse, AI leverage and margin fall below the stated shared-report model.
- If owners treat the weekly drop as generic content, free AI and PLR alternatives weaken both the base subscription and the upgrade's retention value.
- The add-on depends on a viable base Shovel payer flow; an in-product upgrade is not an independent acquisition channel.

## Improvements separate from score
- A fixed outcome dictionary, consent language, ownership rules and retention/reuse rights would make the claimed analytical asset more definite.
- A stated report cadence, minimum comparable-cohort threshold and exclusion rules would bound relevance and buyer expectations.
- A defined automated intake and anonymization path plus an analyst-exception rule would clarify whether human review remains proportionate at scale.
- A documented non-founder-dependent route to the base Shovel payer would separate buyer acquisition from the upgrade checkout.

## Unresolved questions
- Which specific owner and member outcomes are collected, and can they be measured consistently across communities and niches?
- Who controls, may reuse and may transfer the submitted outcome records, including consent withdrawal and privacy obligations?
- What comparison is sufficiently actionable to change a community owner's planning decision rather than merely be interesting?
- What periodicity and cohort size make a $20/month layer credible to the payer?
- Can the required comparability checks stay shared work as participation diversity increases?
- Which established channel reaches enough paid Shovel owners without relying on Jack's continuing personal selling?

## Overall interpretation
This is a credible stickiness layer for a functioning Shovel host: it adds a recurring reason to stay, an accumulating data asset and a genuine scale mechanism. Its fit is conditional rather than standalone because the host payer flow, permissioned comparable data, upgrade value and bounded human interpretation are all unresolved; the data layer also introduces material friction, margin and founder-independence trade-offs.
