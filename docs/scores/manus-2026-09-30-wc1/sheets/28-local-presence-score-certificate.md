## Assessment frame
- **Buyer and payer:** Host product: an agency's client-value/resale offer or a chamber's member-service offering. In WC1 the payer is an agency buying a $99 annual assessment for an SMB client; the SMB is the certificate recipient and display user. Chamber distribution is an alternative, not an additional proven payer.
- **Offer and delivery:** A fixed, published local-presence rubric produces an earned score and displayable certificate. AI gathers public inputs, proposes scores and generates the certificate; an assessment operator checks material facts and handles appeals before the agency or chamber supplies it to the SMB.
- **Price hypothesis:** $99 per business annually, with possible multi-client agency seats; this is an unvalidated catalog hypothesis rather than evidence of agency margin or willingness to pay.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Annual recertification as business profiles or criteria change, plus multiple sites or clients assessed through the same agency/chamber host.
- **Founder role:** Proposed steady state assigns evidence checks and appeals to an assessment operator; Jack maintains product standards only occasionally (assumed 0–2 hours weekly) and does not host a community or provide bespoke client work.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Trophy — an SMB can display an earned local-presence certificate; the status payoff depends on the standard being understood and trusted by observers.
- **Founder fit:** conditional

## Material assumptions
- Agencies or chambers see sufficient member/client value or resale margin to administer a $99 annual certificate without founder-led selling.
- The rubric uses clear, measurable and defensible criteria and makes no unsupported accreditation claim.
- Evidence collection, corrections and appeals remain bounded enough for an operator-led process to have worthwhile margin at the proposed price.
- Recipients and their audiences recognize the issuer or score sufficiently for a displayed certificate to confer status.
- Assessment observations can be permissioned, consistently defined and reused to form a transferable benchmark layer; no such proprietary dataset is currently demonstrated.
- AI can reliably perform public-input collection, scoring proposals and certificate production while human verification remains limited to material exceptions.

## Sensitivities
- If the issuer is not recognized, the Trophy payoff weakens, reducing renewal, No Brainer and agency-channel appeal together.
- If verification, corrections or appeals are frequent, the proposed annual price may not support margin, AI-led fulfillment or operator independence.
- If outcome observations cannot be permissioned and normalized, the benchmark layer does not create Gold or meaningful scale gravity.
- The result is sensitive to whether agencies, rather than chambers or SMBs directly, are the actual payer and whether they have a resale or retention incentive.

## Improvements separate from score
- A fixed criteria and evidence specification, including a bounded correction and appeal path, would reduce bespoke assessment work without changing the current score basis.
- An issuer-recognition design tied to a meaningful, observable local-presence standard would make the certificate's Trophy value less dependent on a bare badge.
- A defined permission, taxonomy and comparability model for observations would make the proposed benchmark layer more transferable and scalable.

## Unresolved questions
- Which host is the primary payer route—agency, chamber or another existing audience owner—and what margin or retention job makes it adopt the offer?
- What external audience, if any, recognizes the score sufficiently for an SMB to gain status by displaying it?
- Can a defensible local-presence rubric be verified with bounded human work at $99 while avoiding accreditation or outcome claims?
- Which assessment observations can be legally permissioned and standardized into a useful benchmark rather than a noisy score archive?
- How often would agencies or SMBs recertify once the score is already displayed?

## Overall interpretation
This is a conditionally coherent Trophy ingredient with a concrete annual repeat mechanism and a systemizable delivery design, but its central value is issuer credibility rather than certificate generation. The named agency/chamber route, recognition, verification economics and permissioned-data layer are material unproven dependencies; the concept remains a hopper item and this assessment does not evidence demand, a channel agreement or an operating margin.

## Scope note
This is an independent concept-fit assessment of the stated WC1/WC1.1 scenario. It is not evidence of demand, customer results, margins, partner access, or a launch decision. Existing status remains unchanged.
