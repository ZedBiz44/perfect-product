## Main findings

- **09 Member Wake-Up Campaign and 20 Recurring Customer-Value Kits: 17/20**, seven strong fits, no zeros, and a concept-fit shortlist signal under their bounded shared-asset/operator assumptions. These are distinct campaign variants, not permission to displace the current small-item direction. If Jack must regularly deliver or approve each edition, the founder score falls and the signal disappears.
- **01 Shovel, 02 member-action unit and 21 themed-day add-on: 17/20**, but founder fit remains conditional. The described Shovel pipeline still assigns Jack recurring selection/review. A documented editor handoff matters more than another point on paper.
- **06 Platform Weather:** nine bars assessed, eight strong fits. The missing seller-to-payer acquisition route prevents a total; this is not a low-score rejection.
- Many kits have a plausible useful/reusable shape but lack a usable AI/human task split or a repeatable route to paying licence customers. Downstream client audiences, research communities and lists of potential sponsors were not automatically counted as acquisition channels.
- Portfolio structures can earn credit for transferable assets or shared production while still lacking a specific buyer experience, price/value exchange and delivery model. No hypothetical successful product was inserted to fill those gaps.
- **18 Award-Submission Factory has zeros on bars 5 and 6** because the original award-submission occasion is contradicted by its source. This is a design conflict, not a penalty for missing sales. Other dead/archived candidates remain so.

### Consistency decisions

The review considered the rubric’s broad continuing one-time-buyer alternative, not just subscriptions. Cash This Week and the Board report receive strong recurrence/continuing-market fit on that basis; narrowly scoped specialist licences retain partial fit where the same payer can reuse one purchase extensively. Acquisition remains a separate bar.

The add-on in 21 inherits the explicit Shovel editorial role and is conditional. The broader campaign version in 20 is judged under an explicitly labelled steady-state trained-editor assumption; this is not a claim that such a handoff already exists. Its sensitivity identifies exactly how that score changes if the assumption fails.

Sponsor prospect categories do not establish sponsor acquisition: both 23 and 31 retain U on bar 8. Similarly, 03 and 32 do not receive channel credit merely because source research took place in agency groups. The 30 combined Shovel/report variant has meaningful named AI work in its base asset and added human evidence work, supporting partial C3PO fit; the standalone benchmark 22 has no usable task split and remains U.

## Reading the result

A shortlist signal requires all ten numeric, at least 17/20, seven strong fits, no zeros and founder constraints holding. Conditional or unclear founder fit is not a confirmed signal. Earlier rejected/archived status separately prevents advancing an idea on the strength of this review.

The next useful information is the specific missing detail or sensitivity listed on each sheet—not another general score average. Strong structural mechanics are not proof of a wanted underlying product.
