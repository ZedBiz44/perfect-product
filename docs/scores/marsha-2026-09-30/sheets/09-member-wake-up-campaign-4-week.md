## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Paid community owners (local-business / marketers / practical operators).
- Promise and delivery: Fixed four-week campaign to wake quiet members with measurable weekly results — not an endless content vault. Weeks Return → Complete → Prove → Continue; same campaign to multiple owners; track posts/actions; subscription only after proof.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: Founding pilot US$99–$149 for four weeks. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Shared campaign development and exception review; community owners post, interact and report their own results.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Harvest Gala primary; Six Pack of Beer Desire supporting weekly wins.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: four-week standard campaign with only the source’s light terminology/example substitutions, not custom campaigns. A2: shared agents generate candidates/materials and a trained operator uses written standards; Jack reviews exceptions. A3: existing paid communities have an owner capable of posting.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
