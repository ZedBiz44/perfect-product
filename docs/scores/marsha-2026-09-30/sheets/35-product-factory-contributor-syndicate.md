## Product and assumptions

- Internal product-manufacturing capability first; downstream distributors are possible. There is no customer offer or paying customer selected at the factory level.
- Repeatable pipeline: market/problem research → asset → campaign → QA → packaging → test. Optional contributor expertise feeds shared products.
- No customer price at this level; it is a cost centre until a wanted output sells. Do not import revenues from an invented successful product.
- Jack supplies direction and asset-level judgment; AI agents handle research/drafting/creative production, with contributor and human QA inputs.
- No customer satisfaction shape selected at factory level; each output must earn its own assessment.
- Catalog price hypothesis: N/A as customer offer; cost center until a product sells.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: Agent-assisted research, drafting and packaging follow the explicit Z3 production pipeline, with humans checking rather than rewriting every unit.
- Assumption: A2: Contributor IP rights can be licensed or assigned; the factory retains its original systems.
- Assumption: A3: Reuse is assessed at the shared production-process level; an output’s sale, margin or acquisition channel is not assumed.
