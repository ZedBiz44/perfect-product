## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Small-to-mid paid Skool / membership / community owners; local-business coaching groups; optionally large free FB AI group owners (secondary).
- Promise and delivery: Give an existing audience owner one small, fresh, useful idea each week they can share with their people under their own name. Weekly drop = one concept + 2–4 engagement posts (including comment-to-get) + small supplementary pack; niche editions of the same format; Jack/AI produce, owner posts.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: Original concept: $19–$29/month, anchor $27. Catalog proposal: ~$29–$49/month founding. Both remain unapproved. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Select and check the shared weekly concept; no posting or hosting inside customer groups.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Six Pack of Beer Desire; supporting Tool in the hand.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: one standard edition serves many owners, not individual custom copy. A2: an editor can eventually learn the review criteria, but the source still makes Jack the regular selector and reviewer. A3: commissions, AI cost and bounded support fit within the proposed price at a viable subscriber base.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
