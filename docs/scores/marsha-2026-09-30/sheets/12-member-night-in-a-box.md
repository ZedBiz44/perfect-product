- Promise and delivery: Night-in-a-box for Skool/Facebook/chamber owners: theme, mission, guest prompt, photo moment, run-of-show — owner runs it; Jack never hosts. Sell per night or seasonal pack; staff/VA of owner executes.

- Founder role: Supply event assets only; the customer’s owner, staff or VA runs the room and the event.
