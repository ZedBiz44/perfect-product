## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Agencies and local marketers with dormant client/customer lists.
- Promise and delivery: Customer reactivation packaged as a short campaign owners/agencies can run to get cash this week. Fixed reactivation messages/workflow for dormant lists; not differentiated enough from existing GHL practice unless niche-specific.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: ~$99–$199 per campaign pack. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Supply the fixed campaign; buyer or agency handles lists, permissions, sending and resulting sales.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Harvest Gala; supporting Tool in the hand.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: fixed reusable messages/workflow only, not managed sending. A2: buyers have a usable dormant list and lawful permission. No new niche differentiation, AI task split or acquisition channel is added.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
