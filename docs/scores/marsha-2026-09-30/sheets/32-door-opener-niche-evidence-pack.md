## Product and assumptions

- Niche-focused agencies pay; their prospects receive the free win.
- A fixed niche pack combining a practical free-win script/checklist with defensible proof points that help an agency begin a sales conversation.
- $149–$299 per niche pack is a hypothesis. Additional niches or occasional updates are the limited repeat-purchase ladder.
- Jack’s expertise may inform an initial shared pack; the proposed boundary excludes diagnosing or fulfilling individual agency prospects.
- Primary: Tool in the hand. Supporting: none required; better confidence is not treated as an earned Trophy.
- Catalog price hypothesis: ~$149–$299 per niche pack.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: Evidence is lawful, accurate and reusable across the selected niche, not fabricated outcome claims.
- Assumption: A2: The sold unit remains a fixed pack; the agency handles prospect use and any implementation.
- Assumption: A3: Multiple-niche demand is optional and irregular, not a subscription disguised as certainty.
