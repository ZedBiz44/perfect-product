## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Paid community owners whose pain is lurkers / empty room / churn.
- Promise and delivery: Reframe the weekly drop as one member-action unit (mission + comment-a-word + homework) instead of a tip vault. Same weekly cadence as Shovel; success measured by members completing an action and owners reusing next unit.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: Same band as Shovel (~$29–$49/mo). Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Review the shared mission format and content; the paying owner runs member interaction.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Six Pack of Beer Desire; supporting Tool in the hand.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: inherits the documented Shovel shared AI-production and partner-distribution model, since the entry explicitly calls this the same weekly drop reframed. A2: missions remain short and usable across one edition; no personal homework marking by Jack.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
