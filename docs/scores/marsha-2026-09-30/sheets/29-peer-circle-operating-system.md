## Product and assumptions

- Group owners pay for a licensed peer-circle OS; their members are users, not assumed payers.
- Prompts, missions and a scoreboard; the owner’s staff runs the room. The promise is peer participation without inventing the format.
- $49–$199/month per community is an unvalidated catalog hypothesis. Continuing missions and operating support, rather than the licence label alone, offer a reason to renew.
- Jack sells the reusable system and does not host member communities. Product upkeep and licence support are transferable routine jobs.
- Primary: Belonging without hosting. Supporting: Tool in the hand for the owner.
- Catalog price hypothesis: ~$49–$199/mo seat licence per community.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: A licence includes a useful ongoing mission/format refresh, not a permanently static prompt download.
- Assumption: A2: The buyer already has a functioning community and an operator able to facilitate; Jack supplies no engagement-rescue service.
- Assumption: A3: Rights to original missions and operating materials are retained and licensable. No AI production process or acquisition route is silently added.
