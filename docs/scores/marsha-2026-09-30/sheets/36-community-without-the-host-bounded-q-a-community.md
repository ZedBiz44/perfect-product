## Product and assumptions

- Agency owners or AI-operations practitioners are proposed paid members; the target buyer remains explicitly undecided.
- Paid community, daily ops material/FAQ support and Jack’s scheduled weekly 1–2-hour Q&A; the archive red-flags VA-as-social-engine as already failed.
- Proposed ladder: $7–$15 letter, $29–$49 community, $97–$149 playbook, $1.5k–$2.5k advisory. Recurring shared Q&A/content is the base; advisory is a separate labor-heavy risk.
- Jack delivers scheduled Q&A and escalation judgment. Agents produce digests/FAQ responses; VAs can handle logistics but their proposed social-engine role is contradicted.
- Primary: Belonging without hosting. Supporting: Event Excitement for scheduled Q&A, Tool in the hand for usable playbooks.
- Catalog price hypothesis: $7–15 letter; $29–49 community; $97–149 playbook; $1.5–2.5K advisory.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: Judge the archived design with its correction, not a redesigned partner-owned community.
- Assumption: A2: Bounded Q&A is allowed founder activity; it is not automatically treated as daily hosting.
- Assumption: A3: Human community logistics and agent FAQ/digest work can operate competently, but a working peer-social engine is not assumed away.
