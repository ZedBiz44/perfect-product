## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Rural real-estate agents, land lenders, close partners (payers); end buyers get the worksheet.
- Promise and delivery: Region-specific rural/acreage pre-offer system sold to agents/lenders who already meet buyers at the moment of need. Lead-capture page, property-visit scorecard, professional-question list, auto follow-up; fixed no-customization founding licence.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: Founding licence ~$497–$2,000 + possible ~$97/mo. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: License a fixed region-specific system; qualified reviewers check local accuracy, channel owners serve buyers.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Tool in the hand; supporting none required.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: no-customization licence is enforced. A2: factual region updates and specialist review happen at the shared product level. Price and optional monthly payment remain hypotheses, not proven orders.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
