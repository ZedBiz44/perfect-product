## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Agencies with retainer clients who churn on "what am I paying for?"
- Promise and delivery: A weapon agencies fire at the churn moment — one click assembles a white-labeled "here's everything we've done" proof packet from their own data. Triggered when client asks "what am I paying for?"; assembles proof from GHL/data; tracks save/lose for a dataset moat.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: ~$49–$149/mo or ~$199–$499 one-time + updates. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Operate a proof-packet product; agency owns its data, client conversation and retention outcome.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Tool in the hand; supporting Trophy for visible proof.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: assessed version is Mary’s automated evidence packet, not a custom save-call service. A2: standardized data access and lawful use are feasible, but integration support remains meaningful. AI-specific allocation and agency acquisition are not supplied by the source.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
