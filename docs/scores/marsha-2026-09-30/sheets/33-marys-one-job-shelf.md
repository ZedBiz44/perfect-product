## Product and assumptions

- Operators buying a narrow job kit are the intended payers. Agencies/chambers are possible later distributors, not assumed current customers.
- A small self-serve shelf labelled by the job to be completed. Example labels include no-show recovery and review requests; no individual kit is selected here.
- $29–$79 per kit is proposed. Buyers may return for a different job; that is an optional cross-purchase, not an established replenishment cycle.
- Store operation, delivery and catalogue maintenance are delegable; no discovery calls or Jack-delivered service are part of the proposed shelf.
- Intended primary: Tool in the hand. Actual satisfaction and effort depend on the still-unselected kit.
- Catalog price hypothesis: $29–$79 per kit.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: Catalogue contents are original/licensed fixed products, not custom service vouchers.
- Assumption: A2: Jack’s directories offer some relevant placement access, as Mary proposes; traffic and conversion are unknown.
- Assumption: A3: Additional kits are added after demand, not credited as successful inventory in advance.
