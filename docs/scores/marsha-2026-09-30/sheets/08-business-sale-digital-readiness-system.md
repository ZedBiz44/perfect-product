## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Business brokers, succession advisers, accountants, lenders.
- Promise and delivery: Broker-branded digital-readiness system for owners preparing a business for sale. Seller workbook, asset inventory, ops/key-person prompts, broker intake, non-legal transition checklist; fixed-scope licence.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: Founding licence ~$497–$2,000. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: License standardized broker intake materials; broker owns sale advice and seller follow-through.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Tool in the hand; supporting none required.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: fixed-scope, non-legal starter system only; no consulting or credentials collection. A2: maintainable standard workflow can serve multiple broker firms. No assumed recurring licence term or AI split.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
