## Product and assumptions

- Agency-referred small businesses are potential retail payers; agencies may instead buy/resell. The payer/retailer arrangement remains unresolved.
- A self-serve fixed job box offered when an agency declines a below-scope job. Agencies do not implement it.
- $29–$149 per product in the catalog; Manus suggests $29–$99 for the box. Both are unvalidated hypotheses. Repeat need depends on other small jobs.
- Operate the product catalogue and referral delivery; do not turn rejected agency jobs into Jack’s service queue.
- Intended primary: Tool in the hand. The precise box and end-user result are not selected.
- Catalog price hypothesis: $29–$149 per job product.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: The assessed boundary remains a fixed self-serve product, not white-label service fulfillment.
- Assumption: A2: Agencies can retain their client relationship while referring/reselling; the source explicitly leaves channel conflict unresolved.
- Assumption: A3: The structure can reuse original/licensed product IP; no particular successful box is assumed.
