## Product and assumptions

- Relevant tool/supplier sponsors pay; audience owners distribute; members consume the useful weekly item.
- A small Shovel item that remains useful without its sponsor; sponsorship buys association with a relevant voluntary next action, not access to private client lists.
- Catalog hypothesis: sponsor $500–$5,000/month; owner free or $0–$19. Fresh audience actions/exposure provide a possible repeat campaign reason.
- AI performs Shovel shared production. Sponsor sales, suitability checks and account coordination remain operating responsibilities, not assumed automated.
- Primary for members/owners: Six Pack of Beer Desire. Sponsor: Tool in the hand for a relevant promotional placement, with Harvest Gala only if meaningful results can be reported.
- Catalog price hypothesis: Sponsor ~$500–$5,000/mo; owners free/$0–$19.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: The assessed version is the small sponsored weekly item, not the expressly rejected large coordinated service box.
- Assumption: A2: Sponsor relevance can be checked and contact occurs only through a voluntary next step; no automatic list transfer.
- Assumption: A3: Inventory can be standardized across multiple participating owners without custom campaign delivery per sponsor.
