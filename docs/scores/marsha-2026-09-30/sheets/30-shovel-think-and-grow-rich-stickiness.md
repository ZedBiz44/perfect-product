## Product and assumptions

- Existing Shovel audience/community owners are the buyer and payer.
- A weekly small useful item plus a periodic report of useful outcome/benchmark evidence; the report is a retention ingredient, not the main business.
- Shovel base price plus a proposed $10–$30/month increment or annual-report upsell; none is validated. New weekly use and periodic new evidence support repeat demand.
- AI researches and drafts shared weekly units; Jack selects and checks at the asset level. Benchmark evidence requires collection and validation.
- Primary: Six Pack of Beer Desire. Supporting: Tool in the hand for evidence-informed decisions; Trophy only when real attributable outcomes are displayable.
- Catalog price hypothesis: Shovel base + ~$10–$30/mo or annual report upsell.. Unvalidated; not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Repeat-payment mechanism and limitations: see bar 1.
- Practical steady state and competent ordinary execution assumed. Missing sales/tests alone do not cause 0 or U.
- Assumption: A1: Reporting remains a bounded shared ingredient rather than bespoke owner analytics.
- Assumption: A2: Lawful, comparable outcome evidence can be collected with permission; no proprietary dataset currently demonstrated is assumed to exist today.
- Assumption: A3: The report changes a practical owner decision and is short enough to consume alongside the weekly item.
