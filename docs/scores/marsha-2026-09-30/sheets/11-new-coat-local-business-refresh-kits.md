## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Agencies, chambers, franchise HQs (channel); end local businesses.
- Promise and delivery: Fixed "new coat" local-business refresh packages at life moments (new location, new offer, post-renovation, slow-season reset). One finished photographable package; sold to agencies/chambers who resell; GHL only delivers assets; no custom design creep.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: ~$149–$499 per kit to channel; marked up to end client. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Supply fixed refresh assets; agencies/chambers or their clients install and show them.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Coat of paint; supporting Tool in the hand.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: a finished standard kit rather than custom rebranding. A2: capable channel customers can apply ordinary brand substitutions themselves. AI production and an upstream route to the channel payer are not assumed.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
