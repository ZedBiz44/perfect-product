## Product and assumptions

- Buyer and payer: Marketing agencies / GHL-style agency owners who struggle to get clients.
- Promise and delivery: Give an agency one tested, niche-specific free win it can offer a prospect to open a sales conversation. Packaged prospect-facing win (e.g. Google profile improvement for dentists) with evidence of what works; agency runs it; Jack does not take sales work.
- Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism: ~$99–$249 one-time per niche pack, or low monthly refill of new niche wins. Repeat rationale is recorded in bar 1. All prices are unapproved hypotheses, not actual purchases.
- Founder role: Supply a reusable niche win; the agency implements the prospect offer and sells its own service.
- Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes: Tool in the hand; supporting Six Pack for optional refills.
- Material assumptions (labelled): A1: one standardized niche pack is reused rather than diagnosing each prospect. A2: evidence upkeep can be shared at niche level. No AI task allocation or ZedBiz acquisition channel is invented.
- Assessment basis: competent execution in a practical steady operating state; lack of launch, interviews, purchases or experiments alone is not failure. No other reviewer score set was read to anchor this assessment.
