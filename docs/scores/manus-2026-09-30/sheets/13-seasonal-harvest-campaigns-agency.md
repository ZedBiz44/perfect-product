## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The catalog names **agencies and associations** as buyers; each is presumed to pay the stated per-season, per-niche-edition license price. The described resale/markup mechanism is specific to agencies; the entry does not state an equivalent association use or funding route. Agencies' local-business clients are intended downstream recipients, not the payer to this product.
- **Promise and delivery:** A ready seasonal campaign—examples include winter reactivation, spring open, and back-to-school—in a niche edition is sold once per season. An agency marks it up to relevant clients; the intended harvest is results photos/case numbers. The entry does not define campaign components, license terms, delivery format, deployment work, or promised business outcome.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** **~$199–$997 per season per niche edition**, expressly an unvalidated catalog hypothesis. Repeat is a calendar mechanism: an agency can buy a new seasonal campaign and/or niche edition in a later season, then resell it to its existing clients. It is not a subscription or a proven renewal.
- **Founder role:** **Unknown.** Neither the entry nor its cited source assigns campaign creation, quality control, support, operations, or exception handling to Jack, AI, staff, or agency partners.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Harvest Gala.** The intended seasonal pile of client outcomes, photos, or case numbers fits the named shape. No supporting shape is expressly promised; the prebuilt campaign may provide a limited *Tool in the Hand* effect for the agency, but that is an inference rather than a catalog claim.

### Material assumptions

1. **[Assumption for bars 2, 3 and 10]** “Sold” campaign editions are reusable licensed packages, rather than rebuilt for each agency/client, and the seller retains the underlying campaign IP. This is consistent with the cited source saying “we sell the license,” but the entry supplies no license terms.
2. **[Assumption for bars 1, 5, 6 and 9]** Agencies can offer a seasonal campaign to their existing clients and those clients will execute enough of it to produce the intended seasonal result. No purchase, uptake, outcome, or willingness-to-pay evidence is claimed.
3. **[Source fact / constraint]** The listed price is unvalidated; the entry explicitly says it is not evidence of willingness to pay. The cited source likewise asks whether agencies sell seasonal add-ons and what they charge.
4. **[Unknown, not assumed]** The AI/human task split, a repeatable route by which this seller reaches agency/association payers, and a founder-independent operating model are absent. These omissions produce U scores rather than being filled in by assumption.

## Result

- **Coverage /10:** **7/10** numeric bars assessed; bars **4, 7, and 8** are U.
- **Concept-fit total /20:**
- **Reason total is blank:** Rubric v1.1 requires a blank total when any bar is U.
- **Strong fits /10:** **4/10** — bars 1, 2, 3, and 10.
- **Every zero and its conflict:** **None.** No bar is scored 0: missing validation or design detail is not treated as a model conflict.
- **Shortlist signal under rubric v1.1:** **No.** The assessment is incomplete (three U scores), has no numeric total, and has four rather than at least seven strong fits. This is not a status change from hopper.
- **Assumptions that could change scores:** Retained reusable-IP rights and a genuinely fixed campaign package support bars 2, 3, and 10. Evidence that delivery is client-specific could lower those scores. An explicit founder-independent operating model, AI/core-work split, and partner/search/reseller route would resolve the U bars; they are not treated as features of the assessed version.
- **Unresolved question:** Can this catalogued offering define a founder-independent, AI-led production/support workflow and a repeatable, incentivised route to agency payers—without turning the campaign library into custom agency service—and do agencies actually have a budgeted seasonal-add-on use case at the stated price range?

## Possible improvement — not part of the assessed version

Document a fixed, license-ready campaign package (including permitted customization and deployment steps), an AI-drafted/human-reviewed production and support workflow, and one agency-access channel with its owner incentive. Test whether a named agency niche buys and resells one seasonal edition. These are **possible improvements only**; they were not credited in the scores above and do not alter the catalog’s hopper/add-on status.
