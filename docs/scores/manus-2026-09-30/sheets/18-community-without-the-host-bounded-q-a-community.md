## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** **Undecided.** The entry names either agency owners or AI-ops practitioners, but does not choose one or identify the actual payer or budget holder. The prospective paying member appears to be the intended payer only as an untested design implication, not an assessment fact.
- **Promise and delivery:** Draft promise: “Weekly live answers + daily ops intel for [niche] — without the guru circus.” Delivery is a free feeder and paid Skool community, scheduled weekly 1–2-hour Jack Q&A, agent/VA-supported daily routines, field notes, playbook/template materials, and an archive.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** An unvalidated ladder: $7–15/month letter; **$29–49/month paid community**; $97–149 playbook tier; $1.5–2.5K/month advisory; annual plans proposed at about 10× monthly. Recurrence is the monthly community/letter/tier access, with ongoing Q&A, ops material, daily community operations, and archive access as the stated renewal rationale. No willingness to pay or renewal is asserted.
- **Founder role:** Jack is the scheduled weekly Q&A event (1–2 hours), reviews escalations (about 30 minutes weekly), and may record short videos; the documented steady-state estimate is 2–3 hours/week. He does not perform daily check-ins, small talk, comment duty, DMs, or onboarding.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Belonging without hosting.** Supporting: **Tool in the hand** (playbooks, templates, configurations) and a bounded **Event Excitement** element (the weekly live Q&A). The primary shape remains at risk because the proposed VA social-engine mechanism is explicitly documented as having failed before.

### Material assumptions

1. **Automation reliability assumption:** The planned community agent can safely and usefully perform routine FAQ answers, welcomes, digests, and escalation triage; this is a design claim, not operating evidence.
2. **Shared-cost assumption:** Enough paid members could share the planned Q&A, VA, agent, and tooling cost to produce a worthwhile margin. No audience size, conversion, retention, or margin is established.
3. **Ongoing-value assumption:** A selected niche would perceive recurring live answers and ops intelligence as useful enough to retain membership despite free AI, free communities, and other information sources.
4. **Channel-access assumption:** A free Facebook group, Skool discovery, letter, and YouTube shorts could reach the eventual payer. The plan has no verified audience, partner permission, or channel-owner commitment.
5. **Transferability assumption:** Recordings, member records, playbooks, and templates could be transferred to a buyer, although the explicit “ask Jack” event makes part of the proposition founder-bound.

### Assumptions that could change scores

- A chosen buyer/payer with a documented recurring pain, budget, purchase trigger, and price-to-benefit case could make bar 6 assessable and could strengthen or weaken bars 1 and 8.
- A genuinely transferable replacement for Jack’s weekly Q&A could change bars 3 and 4.
- A demonstrated non-host-dependent peer-engagement mechanism could change bars 5, 7, and 10; the current record instead documents failure of the VA-as-social-engine mechanism.
- Evidence that agent routines work with exception-only human review, plus a viable shared-cost member count, could strengthen bars 2 and 7.

### Unresolved question

**Which single payer—agency owner or AI-ops practitioner—has a recurring, budgeted problem and a specific trigger that makes $29–49/month for this bounded community an obvious replacement for an existing workaround, and can be reached through a channel Jack can actually access without becoming the host?**

### Possible improvement — not part of the assessed version

Choose one payer and one paid job-to-be-done; specify the existing spend/loss, trigger, and channel access. Separately define a transferable substitute for the “ask Jack” event and a peer-energy mechanism that does not rely on the documented failed VA social engine. These are possible redesigns only and have **not** been scored here.
