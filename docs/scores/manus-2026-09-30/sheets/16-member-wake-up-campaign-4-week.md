## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The **paid community owner** serving local-business owners, marketers, or practical operators is both the named buyer and the intended payer of the founding pilot. Their quiet members are the end users/beneficiaries, not the payer. The same-party payer reading is an inference from the cited proposal to sell the pilot to established owners.
- **Promise and delivery:** A fixed, shared four-week campaign is intended to wake quiet members and generate measurable weekly results through **Return → Complete → Prove → Continue**, rather than an endless content vault. The detailed cited proposal describes ready-to-post weekly material (post, follow-ups, action, resource, results tracker) delivered through a portal/notifications; owners post it in their existing community and report results. The promise is a design claim, not an observed result.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** **US$99–$149** for one four-week founding pilot. The entry specifies a **subscription only after proof**; the cited proposal calls a recurring subscription a replenishment plan if the campaign produces a measurable result. Neither the post-pilot subscription price/cadence nor a renewal result is specified or validated.
- **Founder role:** Jack is designed to provide practical judgment, finished materials, quality control, and exception review—not live calls or community hosting. The cited design proposes AI-generated candidates and system selection using accumulated evidence, with decisions codified for a trained operator. Initial pilot production and quality thresholds remain materially unspecified.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Harvest Gala**—the owner is meant to see a four-week pile of visible member wins/actions. **Supporting: Six Pack of Beer Desire**—each ready-to-use weekly unit is intended to be consumed and acted on rather than stored. These are design-level satisfactions, not verified experiences.
- **Material assumptions:**
  1. **Owner-use assumption:** A paid community owner will post the supplied material and complete the weekly feedback/tracking steps rather than let it sit unused.
  2. **Outcome assumption:** The standardized campaign can create sufficiently visible member reactivation/wins for the owner to perceive value; no result is evidenced.
  3. **Repeat assumption:** Four-week success creates a repeat need for fresh campaigns that is worth a subscription despite free AI, PLR, or owner-created material.
  4. **AI/QC assumption:** AI can generate the routine campaign components to an acceptable standard, while Jack or a trained reviewer handles exceptions rather than routine production.
  5. **Data-rights assumption:** Owners can and will supply usable engagement/outcome data, and ZedBiz can retain it in a transferable performance dataset; consent, rights, and data quality are not specified.
  6. **Acquisition assumption:** Proposed LinkedIn/Facebook promotion and direct messages can reach suitable owners. No channel-owner incentive, secured access, referral, search, or partner route is documented.
