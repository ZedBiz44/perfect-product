## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** Rural real-estate agents, land lenders, or close partners are the proposed payers and users. Their rural/acreage prospects are end buyers who receive the worksheet.
- **Promise and delivery:** A region-specific pre-offer decision and client-acquisition system intended to help a payer attract, qualify, and have a pre-tour/pre-application conversation with a rural-property buyer. The stated components are a lead-capture page, property-visit scorecard, professional-question list, and automated follow-up; the license is fixed and no-customization.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** Founding licence of approximately **$497–$2,000**, plus a **possible $97/month**. The entry expressly calls the price unvalidated. The possible monthly payment has no stated continuing job, service scope, or renewal trigger.
- **Founder role:** The entry specifies no ongoing founder role, handoff, verification, support, or acquisition responsibility. The cited screen calls Jack's rural/farming, commercial-lending, and local-marketing experience a founder-fit input, not an operating model.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Tool in the Hand.** No supporting shape is stated.
- **Material assumptions (labelled):**
  - **A1 — reuse:** A fixed regional version can be licensed to multiple channel owners without per-license custom building. This follows the no-customization design but is unproven.
  - **A2 — payer use:** A payer already sees enough relevant buyers and will use the system for lead capture/qualification. The research describes this value as potential, not observed demand.
  - **A3 — local accuracy:** Local rules and specialist review can be obtained without turning each installation into bespoke work. The cited research identifies this as a red flag, not a resolved mechanism.
  - **A4 — renewal:** The possible $97/month would cover an ongoing useful job rather than merely access to a static system. No such job is specified.
  - **A5 — deployment:** The payer can install and operate the page, scorecard, follow-up, and local content with manageable effort. Delivery, implementation, and support steps are not specified.

- **Assumptions that could change scores:** A paid fixed-scope commitment from a named channel owner, a documented recurring job for the monthly charge, a non-founder operating handoff, an AI-led task split, a credible payer-acquisition channel, and a bounded installation/local-review journey could resolve the U bars or change the partial scores. None is evidence for the catalogued version.
- **Unresolved question:** Which specific rural agent, land lender, or close partner will commit to a fixed no-customization licence, through what repeatable channel can ZedBiz reach that payer, and what ongoing operational job would justify the possible $97/month after local review and implementation?
- **Summary:** Hopper channel-owner hypothesis with partial reusable-tool potential, but four material design details—founder independence, AI-core task split, route to payers, and value-delivery journey—remain unknown; no zero is established.

## Possible improvement — not part of the assessed version

Before building, obtain the screen's required paid (or refundable-deposit) fixed-scope founding commitment from one named channel owner. Document one region, the payer's repeated friction and expected use journey, who supplies/verifies local content, the install/support and founder-to-operator handoff, a genuine renewal job for any monthly fee, an AI-versus-human task split, and the channel through which comparable payers can be reached. This is a proposed validation/design change, not a claim about the current hopper entry.
