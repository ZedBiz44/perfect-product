## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The named channel-side buyers are agencies, chambers, and franchise HQs. The described economic payer is the channel organization at approximately **$149–$499 per kit**, which may mark the kit up to an end local-business client. The end local business is the intended recipient/user and may ultimately bear the marked-up price, but the entry does not specify which channel types actually pay versus include the kit.
- **Promise and delivery:** A fixed, finished, photographable local-business refresh package—a “new coat”—for a stated business-life moment. It is delivered as assets through GHL and is meant to be resold or included by the channel, not produced as custom design work.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** **~$149–$499 per kit to the channel**, then a channel markup to the end client. Repeat is per kit: a channel can buy for multiple client refresh moments and a local business can face a later life moment; neither renewal nor observed repeat purchase is stated. The price is an unvalidated catalog hypothesis, not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- **Founder role:** The assessed design does not assign Jack client-by-client delivery, hosting, or custom design. The operating inference is that Jack initially defines/maintains the fixed kit catalog while a documented workflow, GHL delivery, and trained operations or channel staff deliver routine units. Exact production, approval, support, and exception ownership are not specified.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Coat of paint.** The intended experience is a visible transformation/reveal that the business can photograph and show. **Supporting: Tool in the hand (inference only)** for the channel receiving a ready-made fixed kit; it is not separately promised in the entry.
- **Material assumptions (labelled):**
  - **A1 — bounded scope:** “Fixed,” “GHL only delivers assets,” and “no custom design creep” mean routine fulfilment uses prebuilt/reusable assets rather than per-client creative production. This supports bars 2, 4, 9, and 10; the exact contents are not stated.
  - **A2 — channel transaction:** The channel pays per kit and can resell it to more than one end client, as the entry’s per-kit channel price and markup wording indicate. This supports bars 1 and 8; access and actual resale are unproven.
  - **A3 — realization of the reveal:** The end business can use the delivered finished package with manageable approval/placement effort and receive the stated visual refresh. This supports bars 5 and 9; customer use and outcome are untested.
  - **A4 — no AI task split assumed:** GHL delivery is not treated as evidence that AI creates, delivers, or supports the core unit. No AI/human split is supplied.
