## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** Marketing agencies serving local businesses; the agency is both buyer and payer. Agency staff are the direct users/resellers, while the agency's local-business clients are downstream recipients of the drops or campaigns.
- **Promise and delivery:** Packaged retention drops and seasonal campaigns are licensed to agencies, which mark them up to clients. The cited proposal describes weekly client-facing ideas branded as the agency's and a shelf of campaigns tied to local-business seasonal moments; it does **not** specify the fixed contents, format, implementation scope, licensing terms, or support journey.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** The entry's unvalidated hypothesis is about **$97–$497/month** for an agency licence or **$199+** per-season packs. A monthly licence is the stated recurring route; weekly drops and successive seasonal windows are the proposed reason to renew or buy another pack. No willingness to pay or verified market price is claimed.
- **Founder role:** **Unspecified.** The catalog does not assign production, quality review, support, agency acquisition, sales, or operating handoff to Jack, systems, or trained staff.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** The entry labels **Harvest Gala / Six Pack** but does not select a primary shape. For this assessment, **Harvest Gala** is the primary interpretation for seasonal campaigns and **Six Pack of Beer Desire** is supporting for weekly drops; this is an interpretive classification, not an established buyer outcome.

### Material assumptions

1. **Reusable-pack assumption (inference):** “Packaged drops” and a “campaign shelf” mean a shared, reusable digital/IP unit rather than bespoke work for each agency or local-business client. This supports the partial reuse judgment but is not an explicit scope definition.
2. **Agency-execution assumption (source-supported inference):** The agency, not the licensor, distributes, brands, and implements/resells the material for its clients. The entry says agencies mark up the packages; the cited proposal says agencies push campaigns into relevant client accounts. The time and customization required are unknown.
3. **Repeat-demand assumption (unvalidated):** Agencies see enough continuing retention or seasonal-resale value to keep a monthly licence or return for seasonal packs. The price is expressly a catalog hypothesis, not proof of demand.
4. **No proprietary-results-data assumption:** This score does not credit the separate proposed benchmark/data asset because Entry 10 does not include outcome-data collection, agency opt-in, privacy terms, or a data product.
5. **No AI-operating assumption:** No AI/human task split is stated for this catalogued version. AI use is therefore not assumed for Bar 7.
