## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The payer is an unspecified **sponsor** (the related source describes a relevant tool company or supplier). Audience owners distribute; their members receive the weekly item. Owners are proposed to receive it free or at **$0–$19**. The entry does **not** name a sponsor vertical, the sponsor's exact desired action, or a specific sponsor buyer.
- **Promise and delivery:** A sponsor funds a useful weekly Shovel unit so an audience owner can share it free or more cheaply. The unit is intended to be useful without the sponsor logo. The inherited Shovel design is a practical concept, 2–4 ready posts, and a small supplementary pack, delivered for owners to post. The entry says to prove owner use first, then sell sponsor access to the resulting action, while avoiding the rejected coordinated service-box model.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** Sponsor **~$500–$5,000/month**; owner **free/$0–$19**. The monthly sponsor payment is the stated repeat mechanism, funding successive weekly units. Both the price and sponsor willingness to renew are unvalidated hypotheses, not market evidence.
- **Founder role:** In the cited Shovel pipeline, AI agents research, draft, adapt, design, QC, and ship; Jack selects the concept, checks/polishes it, adds practical experience, and approves shipment. The described sponsor layer does not assign sponsor acquisition, relationship management, action measurement, or reporting to a person or system.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Six Pack of Beer Desire** — a small weekly item intended for prompt use and a second payoff later in the week. **Supporting: Tool in the Hand** *(inference for the owner/end recipient)* — ready-to-post materials may make an owner more capable. The sponsor payer's own experienced result is not defined, which causes bar 5 to be U.
- **Material assumptions (labelled):**
  - **A1 — inheritance assumption:** Because entry 34 calls for a “weekly Shovel unit,” this assessment uses the cited Shovel unit and its AI/human workflow as the delivery design. The entry itself does not restate that workflow.
  - **A2 — sponsor-fit assumption:** A relevant supplier could value an audience member's voluntary next action. No sponsor category, action, conversion measure, or commitment is documented.
  - **A3 — non-service assumption:** The assessed version remains a funding layer on a shared weekly item, not the superseded model that selects agencies, coordinates distribution, manages opt-ins, and reports results.
  - **A4 — reuse assumption:** One niche edition can be distributed to multiple owners without bespoke sponsor integration. This is necessary for the reusable-delivery reasoning, but is not specified for the sponsor layer.
