## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** A paid community/membership owner already buying Shovel — with small-to-mid paid Skool owners the source's stated beachhead — is inferred to be both user and payer. Their members are downstream users, not payers.
- **Promise and delivery:** An optional **weekly themed-day plan** gives the owner copy-ready posts and assets: Monday prompt/poll, mission and day-3 check-in, Friday wins thread, live-call topic/run sheet, welcome and re-engagement scripts, an email-all message, and graphics/templates. It is delivered with the core drop by email and vault; the owner copies and posts the pieces in their group.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** The catalogued price is an unvalidated **order bump or +$10–$20/month on Shovel**. The recurring design is a new weekly kit every cycle. The cited concept instead proposes a `$47–$79/month` Pro bundle including the kit and leaves a stand-alone kit price as a test; it does not validate or supersede this entry's `$10–$20/month` hypothesis.
- **Founder role:** Jack selects the weekly theme, reviews/polishes the one standout piece, spot-checks the remaining kit, and gives final approval. The cited estimate is about 1.5 additional weekly hours; he does not host the buyer's group or run its call.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary — Six Pack of Beer Desire**: a fresh usable weekly unit arrives repeatedly. **Supporting — Tool in the Hand**: copy-ready prompts, scripts, and a run sheet let the owner operate a week of engagement.
- **Material assumptions:**
  1. **A1 — Shared-edition delivery:** Each weekly kit is produced once per niche edition and can be delivered to multiple owners without customer-specific rewriting.
  2. **A2 — Recurring billing is actually offered:** The catalog's explicit `+$10–$20/mo` option, rather than a one-time order bump alone, is the assessed payment path.
  3. **A3 — Core-buyer access:** Shovel obtains a sufficiently reachable buyer base so a checkout, email, or vault cross-sell can reach it; no such base is yet evidenced.
  4. **A4 — Planned AI workflow works at suitable quality/cost:** Agents can draft, design, format, QC, and deliver with human editorial review remaining shared per weekly edition rather than per buyer.
  5. **A5 — Use is feasible:** Owners accept the disclosed manual posting, replies/DMs, and optional live-call work required to get the promised engagement value.
  6. **A6 — Rights are transferable:** The vault, templates, and graphics are owned or suitably licensed so their accumulated IP can be transferred with a business.

## Result

- **Coverage /10:** **10**
- **Concept-fit total /20 (blank if any U):** **16**
- **Strong fits /10:** **6**
- **Every zero and its conflict:** **None.** No described-model conflict was identified; unproven demand and operating results are limitations rather than zeros under rubric v1.1.
- **Shortlist signal under rubric v1.1:** **No.** All bars are numeric and there are no zeros, but `16/20` and six strong fits fall below the required `17/20` and seven strong fits.
- **Assumptions that could change scores:** A2 most directly changes bar 1. Failure of A3 would weaken the cross-sell route in bar 8. Failure of A4 or A1 could lower bars 2, 4, 7, and 10. If the daily operator work in A5 is materially heavier than the buyer expects, bars 5 and 9 weaken. A6 affects bar 3.
- **Summary:** **16/20 concept fit: a well-specified, AI-batched weekly cross-sell with credible reusable delivery, but not a shortlist because low-price buyer value, core-buyer access, and willingness to pay against free alternatives remain untested.**
- **Unresolved question:** **Will a named paid community owner use the kit week after week and pay `$10–$20/month` for it rather than free AI/templates or their own content, while accepting the manual posting and hosting work?**
- **Possible improvement (not assessed):** Run the already-proposed single-edition sample and checkout test with a clearly named paid-owner segment, a distinct monthly kit price, and measured use (posts deployed, owner time, and renewal/pre-order choice). This is an evidence-gathering change, not part of the assessed catalog version.
