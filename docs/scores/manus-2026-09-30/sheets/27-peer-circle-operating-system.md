## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The named buyer is a **group owner** seeking peer energy without having to invent it. The entry does not distinguish the individual operator from the organization/community that would pay, so this assessment treats the owner or owner’s organization as the payer (**inference**). No owner segment, community type, budget holder, or existing spend is named.
- **Promise and delivery:** A B2B2C operating structure: license prompts, a scoreboard, and missions to group owners; the owner’s staff or VA runs the room instead of Jack. The promised result is peer energy and belonging without Jack acting as community mayor. The entry does not specify implementation format, onboarding, refresh cadence, support, or measurable outcome.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** **~$49–$199/month per-community seat licence** (**unvalidated catalog hypothesis**). Repetition is the monthly licence for continuing use; the assessed entry does not state fresh weekly assets, updates, a renewal trigger, or evidence that continued access alone will retain payers.
- **Founder role:** Jack sells the system; the buyer’s staff/VA runs the community. The entry intentionally removes Jack from member hosting. It does not allocate content creation, customer success, support, implementation, or ongoing sales after initial licensing.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Belonging without hosting** (explicit). **Supporting: Tool in the hand** (**inference**) for the group owner receiving an operating structure rather than an invented-from-scratch program.
- **Material assumptions (labelled):**
  - **A1 — Existing community:** A purchasing group owner already has a real community and staff/VA capacity. The entry calls for a licence “per community” and owner staff/VA, but does not state activity level or staffing.
  - **A2 — Standardized implementation:** The prompts, scoreboard, and missions can be licensed with no material bespoke design, installation, or ongoing intervention by Jack. This supports Bar 2; it is not stated.
  - **A3 — Renewal:** Continued licence access is valuable enough for a monthly payment even though no content-refresh cadence, results mechanism, or retention evidence is specified.
  - **A4 — Participation:** Structured prompts, a scoreboard, and missions can prompt useful peer interaction when implemented by the owner’s team. The cited archive records a different VA-as-social-engine arrangement as failed, so this is not treated as proven.
