## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** **Not established.** The entry gives two incompatible routes: members might pay if Jack owns the community, or an outside community owner might pay a B2B licence. It names neither a specific payer nor that payer's job, budget, or trigger.
- **Promise and delivery:** Deploy localized specialized AI mentor personas inside a community so its culture can run without founder hosting. A Mary-like agent onboards and routes members; a skeptical agent critiques offers; Jack is curator-only.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** The unvalidated price hypothesis is **membership at about $29–99/month or a B2B licence**. The membership alternative is a stated recurring-payment mechanism; licence term, renewal cadence, and what is licensed are not stated.
- **Founder role:** Jack is described as curator-only / stepping in for high-level strategy. This remains a material founder tie in the source design, and the current direction records Gemini Idea A as conflicting with the no-host constraint if Jack owns the community.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary:** Belonging without hosting. **Supporting:** none documented.
- **Material assumptions (labelled):**
  - **Assumption — technical and quality:** AI agents can be integrated into the chosen community and give safe, useful onboarding, routing, and offer feedback with human escalation rather than routine founder fulfilment.
  - **Assumption — ongoing use:** Members would continue to value a mentor-mediated community enough to renew a monthly membership; no renewal or paid-use evidence is supplied.
  - **Assumption — B2B route:** If an external-owner licence is meant, that owner has a reason and capacity to install it. This is not established and is treated as an unresolved alternate route, not as the assessed design.
  - **Assumption — community dynamics:** AI activity can support genuine peer belonging rather than merely automate posts. The archived VA social-engine attempt failed; it is not evidence that AI resolves that problem.

## Result

- **Coverage /10:** 5/10 numerical bars; bars 3, 6, 8, 9, and 10 are **U**.
- **Concept-fit total /20:**
- **Strong fits /10:** 2/10 — bars 1 and 7.
- **Every zero and its conflict:** **Bar 4 = 0.** A Jack-owned, curator-led community conflicts with the explicit no-community-host constraint; the current direction independently documents Gemini Idea A as conflicting unless reworked. This is a model conflict, not a failure caused only by missing evidence.
- **Shortlist signal under rubric v1.1:** **No.** The assessment has five U scores and one zero, so it cannot meet the complete/no-zero/at-least-seven-strong-fits/17-of-20 signal.
- **Assumptions that could change scores:** A documented shift to a separately specified owner-licence product, with a named payer, self-serve deployment/support boundary, acquisition route, asset ownership, and scale mechanism, could change several results. Those details are not credited to the assessed catalogue version.
- **Unresolved questions:** **Which named payer—member of a Jack-owned community or external community owner—has a recurring, budgeted job that AI mentors solve, and through what repeatable channel will that payer be reached?**
- **Possible improvement (not part of the assessed version):** Treat an external-owner peer-circle operating system as a distinct version (or entry 27): state one owner payer and job, a fixed licence/renewal and installation path, human-exception boundary, ownership of the playbook/data, channel incentive, and a genuine member/data/distribution scale mechanism. Do not assume this resolves the documented no-host conflict until it is designed and tested.
