## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The entry names agencies, associations, chambers, franchise systems, and their SMB client/member bases. It does **not** select which party pays; possible routes include vendor commissions, member economics, agency licensing, and shared savings. SMBs appear to be intended beneficiaries/participants, but that role is likewise not a selected purchase arrangement.
- **Promise and delivery:** Aggregate thousands of SMB buyers, measure repeated spend categories, and then negotiate, white-label, license, manufacture, or acquire better offers as volume grows. This is a discovery-and-procurement structure, not a specified benefit or delivered product for a selected buyer.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** Unset. The four possible economic routes are alternatives, not a price, payer, or renewal/repeat mechanism.
- **Founder role:** Unspecified. The version does not allocate channel recruitment, demand-data collection, vendor negotiation, sourcing, member support, or exception work to Jack, systems, AI, or staff.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** No primary shape is selected. The catalog labels **Tool in the hand / Harvest Gala depending on benefit delivered**, so both are conditional possibilities rather than a defined buyer experience.

### Material assumptions

1. **A1 — channel participation:** A channel owner would voluntarily provide access to enough SMB purchasing demand to aggregate it. This is necessary for the proposed route to buyers but is unproven.
2. **A2 — concentrated, repeatable demand:** Enough participants purchase the same category often enough that their demand is actionable for a supplier. This is necessary for the purchasing-power mechanism; the entry proposes measuring categories but supplies no category or commitments.
3. **A3 — control of the resulting asset:** ZedBiz could lawfully own or retain usable demand data, supplier terms, and relationships. The version does not state this.
4. **A4 — operational transferability:** Sourcing, contracting, reconciliation, support, and exceptions could be documented and delegated. This is not stated and is not assumed to award bar 4.
