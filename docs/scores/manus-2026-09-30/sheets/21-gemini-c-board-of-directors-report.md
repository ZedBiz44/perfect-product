## Product and assumptions

- **Buyer and payer:** The named buyer is a local-business operator with a named bottleneck. The entry implies that the operating business/operator pays the per-report price; it does not name a vertical, budget holder beyond that operator, existing budget, or buying occasion.
- **Promise and delivery:** Productize multi-AI synthesis as a diagnostic. A client submits a problem; the system runs it through diverse AI perspectives and compiles an automated report. Fulfilment is the report only.
- **Price hypothesis and repeat-payment mechanism:** Approximately **$97–$497 per report**, expressly an unvalidated catalog hypothesis rather than willingness-to-pay evidence. The described version has a payment for one report on one submitted bottleneck; it states no renewal, subscription, scheduled re-diagnosis, or repeat-purchase mechanism.
- **Founder role:** The design intends automated report fulfilment and Gemini characterizes it as requiring virtually zero manual fulfilment. It does **not** assign ongoing responsibility for intake quality, perspective/prompt governance, quality assurance, customer support, corrections, or exceptions to Jack, a manager, or a system.
- **Primary and supporting satisfaction shapes:** **Primary: Tool in the hand.** A diagnosis could give an operator a direction for a stated bottleneck. **Supporting label in the catalog: Coat of paint.** A report-only delivery does not itself describe a visible transformation or implementation.

### Material assumptions

1. **A1 — Intake adequacy (assumption):** A buyer can state a bottleneck and supply enough accurate context for the AI process to produce a relevant report. This is necessary for the partial assessments of bars 2, 5, 7, and 9; it is not evidence that reports will be useful.
2. **A2 — Routine automation (assumption):** The described multi-AI run and compilation can be technically automated at a variable cost compatible with the stated price range. This supports reusable-production reasoning only; it does not establish margin, quality, or customer demand.
3. **A3 — Operator payment authority (assumption):** The local-business operator is assumed to be able to authorize a $97–$497 diagnostic. No willingness to pay, existing spend, vertical-specific pain, or urgency is assumed.
4. **A4 — Boundaries remain report-only (source fact):** This assessment excludes consulting, implementation, retainers, or using the report as a lead-in to services, because the entry requires report-only fulfilment and the cited Gemini note warns against service lead-gen.
5. **A5 — No missing mechanism is filled in (assessment boundary):** No ownership/data-rights policy, repeat customer trigger, customer-acquisition channel, manager/QA system, or scale feedback loop is assumed. Their absence produces the relevant `U` scores rather than a redesigned product.

## Result

- **Concept-fit total /20 (blank because any `U`):**
- **Strong fits /10:** 0/10.
- **Every zero and its conflict:** **Bar 1 — Pays more than once: 0.** The catalogued model is a one-time per-report payment with no stated repeat-payment or continuing-buyer mechanism.
- **Shortlist signal under rubric v1.1:** **No.** The record is incomplete (four `U` scores), has a zero, has no strong fits, and cannot meet the all-numeric/no-zero/at-least-seven-strong-fits/17-of-20 rule.
- **Assumptions that could change scores:** A documented standardized intake, automated QA/exception process, low-support economics, a named vertical with a hot paid problem, an opt-in transferable knowledge asset, a periodic repeat occasion, and a partner or owned-audience route could change several scores. None belongs to the assessed version.
- **Unresolved question:** **For which named local-business payer, reached through what repeatable channel, is a $97–$497 report-only AI diagnosis an obvious repeat purchase rather than a one-off curiosity?**
- **Possible improvement — not assessed or scored:** Define one vertical and one bottleneck; specify bounded intake, report content, AI roles, verification and exception rules; make the report-only customer-use path explicit; and, only with appropriate rights, define a transferable de-identified learning asset plus a repeat diagnostic occasion and partner channel. These would be material changes requiring a new assessment.
- **Previous assessment link and changed-score reasons:** **Not applicable.** Per instruction, no prior Grok, Cody, or Mary score sheet was read, compared, or used as evidence.

## Assessment summary

The catalogued offer has a bounded, plausibly AI-led delivery concept, but it is presently a **one-off customized diagnostic report**. The entry supplies neither a repeat mechanism nor an acquisition route, and it does not document the ownership/accumulation, operating handoff, or scale mechanism needed to assess four bars. The six assessable bars are one direct conflict and five partial fits; this is a concept assessment, not proof of demand, price acceptance, margins, report quality, or customer results.
