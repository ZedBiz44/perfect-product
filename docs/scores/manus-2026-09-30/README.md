## Calibration and interpretation

> **Calibration:** These are concept-fit scores for the described catalog version, not customer proof, product approval, commercial authorization, or a claim of demand, payment, renewal, margins, or outcomes. A **2** is a credible specific mechanism, **1** a partial fit with a meaningful limitation, **0** a documented model conflict, and **U** an essential missing detail. Missing evidence alone is not a zero.

Coverage is the completed assessment’s reported numeric-bar count. In line with the rubric, a **total is blank whenever any bar is U**; U is never averaged or converted to zero. The table is in stable catalog-ID order, **not a ranking**. Incomplete entries therefore have no total and no shortlist signal. Even a complete arithmetic result is not customer proof or an approval to build.

The entry 39 sheet records **3/10 coverage**; that completed-sheet result is preserved verbatim here.
