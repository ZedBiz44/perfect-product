## Reading the results

Concept-fit totals are conditional design judgments. Strong fit can use a reasonable assumption at medium or low confidence; it is not evidence of paying demand. Bar 1 includes a broad continuing one-time buyer market, not just subscriptions. Customer effort is assessed against the intended buyer, not against zero effort. AI is assessed at the real production unit, including material human review.

Products, ingredients and structures remain separate. Structures 33–35 and 37–38 are scored only through their explicitly named working application, including structural overhead. Product 35 stays an internal cost centre; no factory subscription is invented. Host add-on channel scores assume a viable parent buyer base rather than claiming one exists. Do not average these scores with earlier versions or treat them as launch approval.
