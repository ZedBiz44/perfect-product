## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Agency reseller payer; assess a $249 reopening announcement kit using fixed editable layouts, with the end business supplying approved photos and details.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$149–$499 per kit to channel; marked up to end client. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Coat of paint
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** New openings, offers and seasonal refreshes create repeat occasions across an agency's clients; each individual business may buy infrequently.
- **Founder/operator model:** A creative operator packages and checks each edition; resellers serve their clients. Jack handles occasional direction, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The fixed reopening kit now separates shared production from reseller customization. No bespoke design is credited.
