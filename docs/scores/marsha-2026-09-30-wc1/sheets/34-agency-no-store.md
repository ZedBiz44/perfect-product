## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Illustrative first handoff: an agency refers a $79 reopening announcement kit to a below-budget prospect; the SMB pays and the agency earns a disclosed referral share.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** $29–$149 per job product. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Agencies regularly reject small jobs, creating ongoing one-time buyer flow; repeat payment from each SMB is less likely.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator manages fulfilment and refunds; agency makes a simple referral. Jack handles rare product decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The SMB payer, referral share and reopening kit resolve the previous undefined handoff. Channel conflict and the customer’s DIY capability remain real limits.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **17/20**. Strong fits: **7/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **holds**. The SMB payer, referral share and reopening kit resolve the previous undefined handoff. Channel conflict and the customer’s DIY capability remain real limits.
- Shortlist signal: **Yes, conditional on the stated design assumptions; not selection or launch approval**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The SMB payer, referral share and reopening kit resolve the previous undefined handoff. Channel conflict and the customer’s DIY capability remain real limits.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
