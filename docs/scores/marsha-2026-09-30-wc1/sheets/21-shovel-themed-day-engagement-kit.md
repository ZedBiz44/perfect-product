## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Existing Shovel owner subscriber pays an additional $15/month for themed-day assets. Assess as an optional layer on product 01.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Order bump or +$10–$20/mo on Shovel. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Weekly themed slots recur, so the add-on supplies fresh scaffolding alongside the base subscription.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator delivers the bundle; Jack's weekly selection remains shared with the core edition, assumed within the same 1–3 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The add-on has an explicit in-product sales route and easy posting journey, but filling more slots can produce noise rather than member value.
