## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Illustrative first shelf item: a $49 review-request kit for capable local operators, drawn from product 14. This is an assessment scenario, not a chosen launch.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** $29–$79 per kit. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Adjacent job kits and additional locations can produce repeat purchases; a single small kit has no inherent recurring payment.
- **Founder/operator model:** A catalog operator maintains products and support; Jack selects occasional additions, assumed 0–2 hours/week once the first kit is stable.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** This is the specified $49 review-request first item, not a score for any imaginable shelf. The selected local-operator job supports the broad one-time-buyer alternative.
