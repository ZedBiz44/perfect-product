## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Agency payer facing a cancellation conversation; assess a $99/month self-serve proof-packet tool using its own client exports. Jack's objections remain.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$49–$149/mo or ~$199–$499 one-time + updates. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Monthly access supports repeated client review and cancellation events; ongoing value depends on having enough client accounts to use it regularly.
- **Founder/operator model:** A product operator handles access and incidents; the agency signs off each packet. Jack has occasional product oversight, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** Self-serve exports clarify the model, but routine account mapping and attribution checks limit AI, margins and scale; Jack’s recorded objection remains.
