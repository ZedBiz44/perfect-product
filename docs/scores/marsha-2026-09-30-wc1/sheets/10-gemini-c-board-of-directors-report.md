## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Local-business owner payer; assess a $197 report for one submitted operating bottleneck, with bounded input and no consultation or implementation upsell.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$97–$497 per report. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** A different bottleneck or quarterly review can prompt another report, but one report may solve the immediate need without repeat purchase.
- **Founder/operator model:** A trained analyst signs off reports using a bounded review standard; Jack handles rare product exceptions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The broad one-time buyer flow remains plausible, but WC1 explicitly adds substantive analyst review for each report. That lowers reuse and AI fit; no consulting is included.
