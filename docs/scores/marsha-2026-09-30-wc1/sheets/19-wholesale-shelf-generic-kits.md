## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Historical killed model: agency pays $297/month for generic seasonal kits licensed across its clients. Assess the generic contents as recorded.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$197–$497/mo. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Seasonal campaign deadlines recur, giving the licence a clear replenishment schedule if the agency keeps deploying kits.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator manages production and access; agency staff serve clients. Jack handles occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The generic shelf remains killed. Its shared AI production is now assessable; commodity content is not automatically a strongly appreciating asset.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **15/20**. Strong fits: **5/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conflict**. The generic shelf remains killed. Its shared AI production is now assessable; commodity content is not automatically a strongly appreciating asset.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The generic shelf remains killed. Its shared AI production is now assessable; commodity content is not automatically a strongly appreciating asset.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
