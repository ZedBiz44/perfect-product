## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Internal production structure, assessed through an illustrative $99 review-request kit sold to local operators via partners. Product economics belong to that example, not a factory subscription.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** N/A as customer offer; cost center until a product sells. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Repeat revenue comes from additional job products and updates sold by the output business; the internal factory itself has no external payer.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operators run research and production, but Jack still selects opportunities and resolves product trade-offs, assumed 2–4 hours/week per active product lane.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The internal factory is assessed only through its documented $99 review-request output. There is no invented factory subscription; new product lanes still demand Jack’s recurring choices.
