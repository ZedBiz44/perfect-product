## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Agency payer running its own prospect outreach; assess one dental-practice profile-improvement pack at $149, inside the recorded range.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$99–$249 one-time per niche pack, or low monthly refill of new niche wins. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Agencies may buy another niche pack or update, but the same niche's opening move can be reused without another payment.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator updates and checks each pack; agency staff do sales. Jack handles occasional product decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week at steady state.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The assessed $149 dental pack can be reused indefinitely; a new prospect is not another paying agency purchase.
