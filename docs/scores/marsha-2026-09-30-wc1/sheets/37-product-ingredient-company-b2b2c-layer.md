## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Illustrative first ingredient: a $297/month CourseFinish action layer sold to a marketing-course company for its learners. Assess that application of the structure only.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** White-label licences ~$197–$997/mo or per-seat. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Successive course cohorts need action support, creating a recurring B2B licence reason tied to the payer's own ongoing sales.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator handles implementation maps; course staff handle learners. Jack is occasional product adviser, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** CourseFinish supplies the previously missing user experience. The score covers this course-action application; per-course mapping limits cross-customer scale.
