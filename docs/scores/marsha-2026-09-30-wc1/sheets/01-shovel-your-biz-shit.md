## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Paid community owner; assess the existing weekly shared drop at $27/month as a working price, retaining the other recorded price hypotheses.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Original concept: $19–$29/month, anchor $27. Catalog proposal: ~$29–$49/month founding. Both remain unapproved. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Fresh weekly member activities replace the owner's recurring job of finding something useful to share; renewal depends on continued use.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator schedules and delivers; Jack still selects the weekly idea and reviews one shared edition. Assume 1–3 hours/week, not per buyer.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** Weekly selection still requires Jack; an operator handles delivery, not the practical editorial judgment.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **17/20**. Strong fits: **7/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conditional**. Weekly selection still requires Jack; an operator handles delivery, not the practical editorial judgment.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- Weekly selection still requires Jack; an operator handles delivery, not the practical editorial judgment.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
