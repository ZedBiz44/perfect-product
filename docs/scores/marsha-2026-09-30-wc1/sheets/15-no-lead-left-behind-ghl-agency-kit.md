## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **15/20**. Strong fits: **5/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conflict**. The historical rejection remains. AI copy and support assistance warrant partial—not strong—AI fit; rule-based routing is not itself AI.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The historical rejection remains. AI copy and support assistance warrant partial—not strong—AI fit; rule-based routing is not itself AI.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
