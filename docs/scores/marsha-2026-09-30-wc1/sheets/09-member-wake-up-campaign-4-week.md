## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Paid-community owner payer; assess the existing four-week campaign at US$129, owner-run and separate from the weekly Shovel format.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Founding pilot US$99–$149 for four weeks. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** A new member cohort or later quiet period creates another campaign occasion; monthly renewal is not inherent in a four-week pack.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator prepares and supports the pack; owner staff run the room. Jack has occasional product oversight, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The WC1 scenario names reuse for another cohort or quiet period, but does not require buying a fresh campaign. I no longer credit my earlier assumed replenishment automatically.
