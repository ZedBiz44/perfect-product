## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Historical archived model: agency-operator member pays $39/month for a room with Jack's weekly Q&A. Optional advisory remains part of the recorded ladder.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** $7–15 letter; $29–49 community; $97–149 playbook; $1.5–2.5K advisory. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Members pay for continuing access, answers and peer interaction, providing a recurring membership mechanism.
- **Founder/operator model:** Weekly Jack Q&A and advisory remain core promised value. VA logistics do not remove his hosting obligation or repair the failed social-engine approach.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The explicit agency-operator/$39 scenario resolves the buyer gap. Bounded weekly Q&A is not treated as daily hosting, but promised Jack appearances and the advisory ladder keep founder fit in conflict.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **11/20**. Strong fits: **1/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conflict**. The explicit agency-operator/$39 scenario resolves the buyer gap. Bounded weekly Q&A is not treated as daily hosting, but promised Jack appearances and the advisory ladder keep founder fit in conflict.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The explicit agency-operator/$39 scenario resolves the buyer gap. Bounded weekly Q&A is not treated as daily hosting, but promised Jack appearances and the advisory ladder keep founder fit in conflict.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
