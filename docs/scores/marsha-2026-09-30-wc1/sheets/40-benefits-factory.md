## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** A relationship-owning business or organization buys a recurring communications package for its audience, customized to its brand and approved facts. Agencies, chambers and associations remain possible payers, not a chosen niche. Swag is supplied through a partner; event planning is a separate bounded package. The prior $297 chamber-discount example is withdrawn; actual pricing is not chosen.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Model not set; likely B2B licence, per-member economics, vendor funding, or blended model. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire for recurring communication; Event Excitement for the optional event package.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** The recurring need to communicate through a newsletter, blog and social posts creates an ongoing payment reason; swag and events add occasional purchases.
- **Founder/operator model:** A content operator manages schedules, approvals and bounded revisions; a partner supplies swag and an event specialist supplies the event package. Assume Jack handles product standards and exceptions, not every client’s content.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1.1 is a customized newsletter/blog/social package, not the previous discount-benefits portfolio. Delegating it does not erase per-client approval work or Jack’s no-custom-client-work constraint.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **14/20**. Strong fits: **4/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conditional**. WC1.1 is a customized newsletter/blog/social package, not the previous discount-benefits portfolio. Delegating it does not erase per-client approval work or Jack’s no-custom-client-work constraint.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- WC1.1 is a customized newsletter/blog/social package, not the previous discount-benefits portfolio. Delegating it does not erase per-client approval work or Jack’s no-custom-client-work constraint.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
