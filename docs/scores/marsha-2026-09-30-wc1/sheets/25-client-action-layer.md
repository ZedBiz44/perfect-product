## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Course creator pays $197/month for a white-label action layer on one repeatable marketing course; learners use it without ZedBiz coaching them.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Licence ~$97–$497/mo white-label, or $29–$99 end-user add-on. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Each cohort needs implementation prompts and updated tasks, giving the creator a recurring licence reason tied to course delivery.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator manages mappings and support; course staff handle students. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The course-company payer and no-coaching boundary clarify the model. Course mapping is still customer-specific work, so founder fit depends on keeping that work bounded.
