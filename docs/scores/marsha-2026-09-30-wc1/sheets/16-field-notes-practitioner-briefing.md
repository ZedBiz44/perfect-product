## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Historical rejected model: agency-operator subscriber pays $12/month for Jack's practitioner letter and reusable artifact. Do not remove its author dependence.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$7–$19/mo or $190/yr (archive table). Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Subscribers pay for a fresh practical perspective and artifact each period; the continuing editorial promise is the renewal reason.
- **Founder/operator model:** Jack's practitioner voice is the product's central promise; routine delegation changes that promise. Regular authorship conflicts with founder independence.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1 explicitly promises Jack’s practitioner judgment and says replacing him changes the offer. That is a founder-independence failure, not simply another editor role.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- WC1 explicitly promises Jack’s practitioner judgment and says replacing him changes the offer. That is a founder-independence failure, not simply another editor role.
