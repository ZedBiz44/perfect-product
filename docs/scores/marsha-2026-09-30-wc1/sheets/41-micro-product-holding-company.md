## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Acquire or revenue-share with an existing small internet-marketing product, then consider adjacent distinctive marketing assets. For one comparable score, use an existing event-planner template product sold to event planners as the first illustrative deal. Keep chamber speaking and WordPress plugins as separate candidate types with different labour and maintenance needs. No target, purchase price, retail price or contract is selected.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Acquisition / revenue-share economics; no retail price set at holding-company level. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand for the assessed template product; Event Excitement may apply to chamber speaking.
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** A template buyer may purchase new editions, adjacent event formats or a broader licence; a one-off download has limited inherent recurrence. Do not assume every acquired IM product is a subscription.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator handles sales and product support, but Jack still selects targets and resolves acquisition/integration decisions. A business dependent on its original speaker may not transfer at all.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1.1 is the event-planner-template acquisition lane only. Conservative deal screening permits a conditional partial margin judgment, not a claim that any actual purchase is attractive.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- WC1.1 is the event-planner-template acquisition lane only. Conservative deal screening permits a conditional partial margin judgment, not a claim that any actual purchase is attractive.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
