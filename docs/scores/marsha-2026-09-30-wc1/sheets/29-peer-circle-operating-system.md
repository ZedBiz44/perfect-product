## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Community-owner payer licenses the peer-circle prompts, missions and scoreboard at $99/month; the owner's staff facilitate, with no Jack-hosted group.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$49–$199/mo seat licence per community. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Recurring circles need fresh missions and facilitation materials, creating a repeat licence reason beyond downloading one playbook.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator maintains the product and bounded support; buyers own the room. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The customer owns the room and facilitates. WC1 now explicitly excludes seller customization beyond bounded product support, improving scale fit.
