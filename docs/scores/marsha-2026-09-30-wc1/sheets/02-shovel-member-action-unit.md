## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Paid community owner; assess a weekly mission plus comment-word and homework at the existing $29–49/month hypothesis.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Same band as Shovel (~$29–$49/mo). Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Each week supplies a new achievable member action, so the unfinished job recurs even after last week's mission is completed.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator publishes and handles access; Jack calibrates mission quality once weekly, assumed 1–3 hours across the edition.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The new journey includes handling comment requests and follow-up, not merely posting a finished item.
