## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Assess the original free diagnostic funnel with $19 DIY fallback and consulting for qualified prospects. It remains an internal-tool hypothesis with a founder conflict.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Free gate; DIY ~$7–49; if productized for others ~$99–$299. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** The gate itself is free; DIY follow-ons and consulting can create revenue, but repeat economics depend on the offers behind the gate.
- **Founder/operator model:** Qualified prospects are routed into Jack-led advisory work, directly conflicting with the recorded no-client-work core constraint.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1 explicitly chooses the consulting funnel. I score the paid path, not just the inexpensive free quiz: bespoke delivery and Jack-led advice fail bars 2 and 4.
