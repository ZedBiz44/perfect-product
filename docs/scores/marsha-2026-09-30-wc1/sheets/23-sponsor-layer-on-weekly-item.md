## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Tool-vendor marketing team pays an illustrative $1,000/month for a defined niche placement in an existing weekly item. Hosts distribute; members remain separate users.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Sponsor pays ~$500–$5,000/mo per niche (wild guess); owners may get free. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Sponsors renew for continued qualified attention, but budget cycles and campaign results make recurrence conditional rather than automatic.
- **Founder/operator model:** A partnerships operator handles routine terms; Jack may still negotiate key accounts, assumed 1–3 hours/week and more during setup.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The assessed payer is the sponsor. WC1 makes human account negotiation and approvals routine; those cannot be treated as rare shared-content exceptions.
