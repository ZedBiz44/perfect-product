## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Shovel owner payer adds $20/month for periodic outcome comparisons. Assess one shared weekly edition plus permissioned benchmark reports.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Shovel base + ~$10–$30/mo or annual report upsell. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Weekly actions plus periodic learning from outcomes create replenishment and review occasions for the combined subscription.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator handles collection and delivery; Jack calibrates content and interpretation at shared-edition level, assumed 1–3 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** This is now an explicit $20 upgrade inside Shovel. The channel score improves, while founder editing and human comparability work remain.
