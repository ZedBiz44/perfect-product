## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Marketing-agency payer; assess a $99/month alert and client-communication subscription covering a bounded set of platforms.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$49–$149/mo. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** New outages and policy changes continually recreate the monitoring and client-communication job, giving subscribers a reason to retain coverage.
- **Founder/operator model:** A monitoring operator verifies incidents and sends notices using an escalation rota. Jack handles occasional policy decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The bounded alert-and-communications model is clearer. Monitoring coverage costs and free alternatives constrain fit, while the buyer no longer has to implement technical fixes to use the promised communication.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **17/20**. Strong fits: **7/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **holds**. The bounded alert-and-communications model is clearer. Monitoring coverage costs and free alternatives constrain fit, while the buyer no longer has to implement technical fixes to use the promised communication.
- Shortlist signal: **Yes, conditional on the stated design assumptions; not selection or launch approval**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The bounded alert-and-communications model is clearer. Monitoring coverage costs and free alternatives constrain fit, while the buyer no longer has to implement technical fixes to use the promised communication.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
