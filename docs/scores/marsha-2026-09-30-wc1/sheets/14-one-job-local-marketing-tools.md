## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Capable local-business owner payer; assess a $99 review-request tool kit as the first example in this broader product family.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$79–$249 per tool; niche packs extra. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Additional locations, niche packs and useful upgrades can generate repeat purchases; the basic review-request job does not require a monthly subscription.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator handles releases and bounded support. Jack informs the product specification and exceptions, assumed 0–2 hours/week at steady state.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The review-request example narrows the earlier tool-family ambiguity. Capable local operators are a broad continuing one-time buyer pool.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **18/20**. Strong fits: **8/10**.

- Founder constraints: **holds**. The review-request example narrows the earlier tool-family ambiguity. Capable local operators are a broad continuing one-time buyer pool.

- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The review-request example narrows the earlier tool-family ambiguity. Capable local operators are a broad continuing one-time buyer pool.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
