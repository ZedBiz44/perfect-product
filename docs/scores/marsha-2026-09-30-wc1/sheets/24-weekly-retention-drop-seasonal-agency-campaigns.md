## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Agency payer adds a $197/month campaign licence to its existing client service, using one weekly retention asset and seasonal campaign editions.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$97–$497/mo agency licence or per-season packs ~$199+. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Agencies have recurring client-value and seasonal selling obligations, creating repeated use occasions for new editions.
- **Founder/operator model:** A campaign operator publishes and supports editions; Jack handles occasional strategy, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The current scenario makes this an add-on through a paying campaign host. That resolves the old channel gap only for the add-on, not standalone cold acquisition.
