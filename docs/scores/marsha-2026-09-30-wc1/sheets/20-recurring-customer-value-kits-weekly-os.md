## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Assess a local-business association as one example payer at $39/month for a finished weekly member-value kit; broader markets remain open.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Founding pilot ~$29–$49 for first month (Z3). Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** The association has a fresh member-value obligation each week, creating a replenishment reason beyond a static library.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator packages and delivers; Jack still calibrates practical usefulness once per shared week, assumed 1–3 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1 restores regular Jack usefulness review. The total remains 17, but the founder qualification now matches Shovel; it is no longer an unconditional shortlist signal.
