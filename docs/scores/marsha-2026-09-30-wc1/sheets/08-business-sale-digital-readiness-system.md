## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Business-broker firm payer; assess a $997 fixed digital-sale-file licence for repeated seller intake, without valuation or transaction advice.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Founding licence ~$497–$2,000. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Additional offices, seller-volume licences and substantive updates can prompt another payment; a one-time licence alone has weak repeat economics.
- **Founder/operator model:** A product operator maintains forms with subject review; brokers own transactions. Jack supplies occasional commercial perspective, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The fixed broker licence has a defined AI workflow, without transferring private seller records into ZedBiz-owned assets.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **16/20**. Strong fits: **6/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **holds**. The fixed broker licence has a defined AI workflow, without transferring private seller records into ZedBiz-owned assets.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- The fixed broker licence has a defined AI workflow, without transferring private seller records into ZedBiz-owned assets.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
