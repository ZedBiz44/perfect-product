## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Tool-vendor payer funds a $1,000/month niche Shovel placement; owners distribute free weekly items. Assess both sponsor and owner tasks.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Sponsor ~$500–$5,000/mo; owners free/$0–$19. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Sponsor renewal follows useful voluntary visits and campaign goals; budget and performance dependency make repeat income less certain than host subscriptions.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator manages distribution and reporting; Jack may negotiate key sponsors, assumed 1–3 hours/week outside startup.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The sponsor-funded combined model has a two-sided adoption problem. Human commercial work is recurring account fulfillment, not just a final content check.

- The sponsor-funded combined model has a two-sided adoption problem. Human commercial work is recurring account fulfillment, not just a final content check.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
