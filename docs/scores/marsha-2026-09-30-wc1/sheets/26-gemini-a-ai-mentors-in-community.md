## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Assess the original Jack-owned paid AI-mentor community at $49/month for marketing operators. Do not silently replace it with product 29's B2B licence.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Membership ~$29–99/mo or B2B licence. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Members pay for continuing answers, resources and interaction, providing a recurring membership mechanism if they keep participating.
- **Founder/operator model:** Jack still owns the room and is the strategic authority; assigning AI mentors or a VA does not resolve the recorded founder-host failure mode.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** This is still Jack’s own AI-mentor community, not the separately scored B2B peer-circle product. Naming moderators does not remove the founder-host problem.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **10/20**. Strong fits: **1/10**.
- Zero conflicts: bar 4: The model retains Jack as the room’s strategic and social authority without a credible transfer of that promise.
- Founder constraints: **conflict**. This is still Jack’s own AI-mentor community, not the separately scored B2B peer-circle product. Naming moderators does not remove the founder-host problem.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- This is still Jack’s own AI-mentor community, not the separately scored B2B peer-circle product. Naming moderators does not remove the founder-host problem.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
