## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Historical killed model: agency payer considers a $299 quarterly award packet for a specific chosen award. Award demand remains unestablished.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Quarterly pack ~$199–$499 (unstated; guess). Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Trophy
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Award seasons and additional clients create occasional repeat demand, not an inherent monthly need.
- **Founder/operator model:** An editor manages submissions and source checks; Jack handles occasional standards decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1 can only earn partial Trophy fit on a hypothetical submission-accepting award. It does not fix the original GHL award, which accepts no submissions, or establish a reason to buy.
