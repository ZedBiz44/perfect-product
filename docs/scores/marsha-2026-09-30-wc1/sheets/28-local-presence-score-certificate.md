## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Agency payer buys a $99 annual local-presence assessment for a client; assume published measurable criteria and an earned certificate, not invented accreditation.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$49–$149 per score / annual recert; multi-client agency seats. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Trophy
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Annual recertification and multiple client sites provide specific repeat-payment occasions as profiles and criteria change.
- **Founder/operator model:** An assessment operator handles checks and appeals; Jack maintains occasional product standards, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** WC1 now includes per-business evidence checks and appeals rather than a licence holder doing all the work. I include that burden in reuse, AI and buyer effort.
