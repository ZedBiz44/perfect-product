## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Historical killed model: agency pays $8/client/month for a monthly narrative and usable asset. The criticism that this sells agencies their own job remains.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$5–$10 per client per month. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** Client reporting recurs monthly, supplying a recurring payment occasion while the agency retains its client base.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator handles accounts and exceptions; agencies approve their clients' narratives. Jack is not the monthly account manager, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** Operator ownership improves founder independence on bar 4, but it does not revive the killed model or remove per-client reporting work.

## Result and interpretation

- Coverage: **10/10**. Concept-fit total: **12/20**. Strong fits: **2/10**.
- Zero conflicts: None.
- Founder constraints: **conflict**. Operator ownership improves founder independence on bar 4, but it does not revive the killed model or remove per-client reporting work.
- Shortlist signal: **No — numeric threshold, zero conflict or founder/status qualification prevents an unqualified signal**.
- Remaining work before commercial reliance: confirm the price, delivery/support boundaries, reusable rights and actual channel access. Numeric concept scores do not settle those operating questions.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- Operator ownership improves founder independence on bar 4, but it does not revive the killed model or remove per-client reporting work.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
