## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Illustrative first created brand: AcreageReady, licensing product 07's regional kit to a rural brokerage at $997 plus optional updates. No brand has been launched.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Per brand founding licences (see #7–8 bands); portfolio economics. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** More brokerage licences and regional updates can repeat revenue; each brand needs its own renewal reason and cannot borrow success from the portfolio label.
- **Founder/operator model:** A brand operator runs fulfilment; Jack still directs opportunity selection and brand decisions, assumed 2–4 hours/week per active new brand.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** The AcreageReady scenario resolves product and journey gaps. It also specifies regular Jack opportunity/brand decisions, rather than only occasional oversight.
