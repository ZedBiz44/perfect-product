## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Membership or chamber owner payer; assess a $99 event pack with staff executing the night and no Jack appearance.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** ~$49–$149 per night pack; seasonal ~$199–$399. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Event Excitement
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** A calendar of events supports repeat purchases and seasonal bundles, but a successful format can also be reused without paying again.
- **Founder/operator model:** Operator maintains packs; the customer's staff host. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week and no event duty.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** I retain strong ease-of-value for the intended staffed organizer: hosting a planned event is the disclosed use, not a surprise implementation project.
