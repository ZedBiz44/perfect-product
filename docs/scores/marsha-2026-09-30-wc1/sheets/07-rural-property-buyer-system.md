## Product and material assumptions

- **Buyer/payer, offer, delivery and working price:** Rural real-estate brokerage payer; assess a single-region pre-offer kit at $997 plus an optional $97/month update licence. Property buyers use the kit.
- **Historical catalog price field, retained:** Founding licence ~$497–$2,000 + possible ~$97/mo. Neither this nor the working price is validated willingness to pay; no unspecified currency is invented.
- **Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Repeat-payment mechanism:** New buyer cohorts support repeat use, but renewal requires substantive regional updates or additional licensed offices; a static checklist is reusable without renewal.
- **Founder/operator model:** An operator maintains editions with a qualified regional reviewer; broker staff handle property-specific advice. Jack is occasional editor, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- Competent steady-state execution, bounded support and lawful reusable rights are assumed. Proposed operators and partners are roles, not assigned staff or signed agreements. No precise profit, renewal rate or automatic distribution is assumed.
- **Marsha’s central qualification:** Single-region scope and a qualified reviewer make this assessable; downstream property buyers are not automatically new paying brokerage licences.

## Sensitivity and improvements — not credited as current features

- Single-region scope and a qualified reviewer make this assessable; downstream property buyers are not automatically new paying brokerage licences.
- A shift from shared editions to seller-delivered bespoke work can lower bars 2, 4, 7 and 10. A genuinely repeatable paid channel can improve bar 8, but an imagined audience cannot.
- An operator handoff that truly removes regular founder delivery can improve bar 4; changing the product’s promised personal expert is a different assessed version.
- Improvements should address the specific limitations in the table and be scored as a new scenario, not silently added to this one.
