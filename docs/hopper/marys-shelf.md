# Mary's shelf

**Status: hopper — catalog structure retained; generic Wholesale Shelf remains dead.**

[Hopper](README.md) · [Source: Mary's Thoughts](../perspectives/mary.md)

## Two different ideas

**Build the shelf, not the box:** Mary's initial angle proposes a small catalog of one-job kits, labeled by the job. The potentially sellable asset is the product line, customer list, and transaction history. This is an unselected idea.

**Wholesale Shelf:** the later proposal for generic seasonal/local campaign kits licensed to agencies was explicitly killed. Its agency distribution structure may be useful, but the rejected contents do not become viable because they sit on a shelf.

## Salvageable piece

Reuse a catalog and existing distribution after a specific item has earned demand. A shelf is a business structure, not evidence that anyone wants the boxes.

## Unresolved

Which one job justifies buying now? Will the same item serve multiple buyers? Does more inventory create repeat use or merely an accumulating library? Avoid building the catalog before answering those questions.
