**Status: hopper — open question, no approval.**

## Salvageable idea

Give an agency one tested, niche-specific free win it can offer a prospect to start a sales conversation. Grok's example is a Google profile improvement for dentists, backed by evidence about what works.

The reusable piece is help with acquiring clients. [Mary's owner research](../perspectives/mary.md#what-ghl-agency-owners-actually-say-listen-first-research-sep-28-2026) identifies getting clients as a recurring complaint.

## Unresolved

- What is meaningfully different from the audits and giveaways agencies already use?
- Would an owner pay for it and actually use it with prospects?
- Can it work without custom fulfillment or handing Jack the agency's sales work?

The September 29 review calls this the remaining agency-shaped question worth investigating. That is a research priority suggestion, not product validation or a decision to replace Shovel.
