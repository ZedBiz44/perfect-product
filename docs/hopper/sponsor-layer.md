# Sponsor layer

**Status: hopper — possible later funding layer; not the current product answer.**

[Hopper](README.md) · [Source: Manus's final correction](../perspectives/manus.md#the-honest-position) · [Grok's sponsor angle](../perspectives/grok.md#2-let-tool-companies-pay-so-owners-get-it-free)

## Salvageable piece

A tool company or other relevant supplier might pay to support a useful weekly item that an audience owner shares. The item must retain its usefulness without the sponsor logo.

## Superseded packaging

The larger sponsor-funded box that requires choosing agencies, arranging distribution, managing opt-ins, and coordinating results failed the cold-beer test. It risks becoming service work. It is not a better version of the weekly item.

## Unresolved

No sponsor commitment is recorded. Prove that owners use the item and that a sponsor values the resulting action before treating sponsorship as revenue. The agency keeps its client relationship; sponsor contact depends on a voluntary next step.
