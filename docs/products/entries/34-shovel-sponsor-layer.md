# 34. Shovel + Sponsor layer

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Sponsored Shovel weekly item
- **Idea:** Useful weekly Shovel unit funded by a sponsor so owners share it free or cheaper.
- **How:** Prove owner use first; then sell sponsor access to resulting action — not a coordinated service box.
- **Buyer:** Sponsors pay; owners distribute; members receive value.
- **Price:** Sponsor ~$500–$5,000/mo; owners free/$0–$19.
- **Tier:** mid–high
- **Shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Status:** mashup
- **Sources:** `docs/hopper/sponsor-layer.md`, `docs/concepts/shovel-your-biz-shit.md`, `docs/perspectives/manus.md`
- **Notes:** Mashup: Shovel + sponsor layer; avoid failed big sponsor-box packaging.
