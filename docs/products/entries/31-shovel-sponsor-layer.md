## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Tool-vendor payer funds a $1,000/month niche Shovel placement; owners distribute free weekly items. Assess both sponsor and owner tasks.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Sponsor renewal follows useful voluntary visits and campaign goals; budget and performance dependency make repeat income less certain than host subscriptions.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Shared content is reusable, but sponsor sales, approval and audience reporting add account-level costs and coordination.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** The publication, host relationships and assignable sponsorship contracts accumulate; no automatic rights to members' data are assumed.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator manages distribution and reporting; Jack may negotiate key sponsors, assumed 1–3 hours/week outside startup.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Owners get weekly relief and sponsors seek a harvest of relevant voluntary interest; delivering both satisfactions reliably creates tension.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A vendor campaign provides a trigger, but $1,000 requires a plausible relevant audience; neither audience size nor sponsor willingness is established.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts units, sponsor variants and reports; humans negotiate and approve sponsor work and verify shared content.
- **Route to the payer:** Community consultants recruit hosts for free useful materials; those hosts may introduce relevant vendors. Both sides require coordinated partner recruitment.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Sponsor agrees terms and approves copy; owner selects and posts; member chooses whether to visit. Value requires several participants to act.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared units can reach more hosts at low production cost, improving the inventory offered to sponsors if permissions and relevance hold.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
