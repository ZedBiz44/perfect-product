# 37. Product Ingredient Company (B2B2C layer)

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Product Ingredient Company / after-they-buy layer
- **Idea:** Manufacture the ingredient that makes somebody else's existing product stickier (implementation, engagement, CS, marketing layer).
- **How:** Licence white-label layers to course companies, associations, SaaS, franchises, directories, consultants.
- **Buyer:** Upstream businesses who lose money when end users don't get results.
- **Price:** White-label licences ~$197–$997/mo or per-seat.
- **Tier:** mid–high
- **Shape:** — (structural; end-user shape varies)
- **Status:** mashup
- **Sources:** `docs/perspectives/z3.md` (Second-Layer Insights), `docs/ingredients.md` (Client Action Layer)
- **Notes:** Combines PLR.me + Hip Kit distribution + white-label + Product Factory patterns stated in Z3.
