## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Illustrative first ingredient: a $297/month CourseFinish action layer sold to a marketing-course company for its learners. Assess that application of the structure only.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Successive course cohorts need action support, creating a recurring B2B licence reason tied to the payer's own ongoing sales.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Shared activity formats are reusable, but mapping each course and maintaining alignment creates setup and support costs.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned delivery layer, task templates and assignable B2B licences accumulate; lesson IP remains with the course company.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator handles implementation maps; course staff handle learners. Jack is occasional product adviser, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A learner completes a useful course task and the course company sees its product being used; this is the first concrete success signal.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** Low completion creates a business problem, but $297 needs useful improvement over existing course worksheets and reminders.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts mapped tasks and routine reminders; humans approve shared mappings and exceptions. No ZedBiz coaching of each learner is included.
- **Route to the payer:** Course implementers and platform consultants could resell for margin and stronger client results; integrations and partner incentives require agreement.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Payer supplies outline, approves mapping, installs the layer and invites learners. This is a bounded setup project rather than instant value.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More learners on one course reduce per-learner cost, but each new course company adds mapping and maintenance work.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
