## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Community-owner payer licenses the peer-circle prompts, missions and scoreboard at $99/month; the owner's staff facilitate, with no Jack-hosted group.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Recurring circles need fresh missions and facilitation materials, creating a repeat licence reason beyond downloading one playbook.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Shared circle formats and assets serve many owners; their staff manage member dynamics and local schedules.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned facilitation IP, template library and licence relationships transfer without requiring Jack to be the community personality.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator maintains the product and bounded support; buyers own the room. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Members completing a shared mission can feel connection, but prompts and scoreboards alone cannot manufacture a healthy peer culture.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An owner seeking member interaction has a trigger; the $99 price must justify value over existing staff practices and free facilitation prompts.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts mission variations and scoreboard summaries; a human checks shared formats. Buyer staff perform member-facing facilitation.
- **Route to the payer:** Community managers and membership consultants could resell for margin; they must see the kit as supporting rather than replacing their service.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Owner configures circles, invites members, runs the mission and reviews participation. Staff time and social coordination remain material.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** One format serves more communities; permissioned aggregate feedback can refine missions without ZedBiz taking on each room.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
