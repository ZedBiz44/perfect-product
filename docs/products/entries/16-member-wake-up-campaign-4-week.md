# 16. Member Wake-Up Campaign (4-week)

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Member Wake-Up Campaign
- **Idea:** Fixed four-week campaign to wake quiet members with measurable weekly results — not an endless content vault.
- **How:** Weeks Return → Complete → Prove → Continue; same campaign to multiple owners; track posts/actions; subscription only after proof.
- **Buyer:** Paid community owners (local-business / marketers / practical operators).
- **Price:** Founding pilot US$99–$149 for four weeks.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Harvest Gala / Six Pack
- **Type:** product
- **Status:** hopper
