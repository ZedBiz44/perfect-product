# 41. Micro-product Holding Company

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Micro-product Holding Company
- **Idea:** Acquire or revenue-share with small proven products that have real customers but weak marketing, distribution, automation, recurring economics, or product ladders.
- **How:** Buy the proof instead of inventing everything; apply ZedBiz packaging, channel distribution, automation, product ladders, and repeat economics; keep winners as standalone micro-brands.
- **Buyer:** Portfolio model; end buyers vary by acquired product.
- **Price:** Acquisition / revenue-share economics; no retail price set at holding-company level.
- **Tier:** portfolio / high
- **Shape:** varies by acquired product
- **Status:** exploration
- **Sources:** Z3 discussion and `briefing/lessons.md` in ZedBiz key-info repo, 2026-09-29.
- **Notes:** Different from #38 Micro-brand factory. #38 creates small brands; #41 acquires already-proven products or brands and improves their economics and distribution.
