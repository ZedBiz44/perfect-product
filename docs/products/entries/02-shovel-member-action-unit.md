## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Paid community owner; assess a weekly mission plus comment-word and homework at the existing $29–49/month hypothesis.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Each week supplies a new achievable member action, so the unfinished job recurs even after last week's mission is completed.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One mission and response pack serves a niche cohort; owners run it. ZedBiz does not coach or chase individual members.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** A licensed mission library, owner list and permissioned completion observations accumulate into transferable product assets.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator publishes and handles access; Jack calibrates mission quality once weekly, assumed 1–3 hours across the edition.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Owner sees a member finish one useful task rather than just like a post; completion supplies a concrete weekly satisfaction moment.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An empty or passive paid group provides a trigger, but paying for another engagement tool needs differentiation from free prompts.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts mission, prompts and follow-up variations; a human checks feasibility per shared mission, while the customer handles member interaction.
- **Route to the payer:** Community setup consultants can bundle a sample mission and earn referral income; access to suitable owner clients is proposed, not established.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Owner posts the mission, handles comment requests, follows up and observes completion. Facilitation creates more effort than simply posting a drop.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** The same mission serves more owners; permissioned completion feedback can improve later missions without requiring individual coaching.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
