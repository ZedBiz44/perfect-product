## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer buys a $99 annual local-presence assessment for a client; assume published measurable criteria and an earned certificate, not invented accreditation.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Trophy. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Annual recertification and multiple client sites provide specific repeat-payment occasions as profiles and criteria change.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse scoring and certificate generation, but verifying evidence, disputed scores and appeals adds per-business work.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned criteria, permissioned benchmark data and issuer relationships accumulate; value depends on credibility rather than the badge image alone.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An assessment operator handles checks and appeals; Jack maintains occasional product standards, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The client can display an earned result, but satisfaction and recognition depend on whether others understand and trust the standard.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A review or renewal creates a trigger, but $99 competes with free audits and an unknown badge may have little status value.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI collects public inputs, proposes scores and produces certificates; a human verifies contested or material facts for each business.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency trainers and chamber service teams could distribute for member value or margin; issuer credibility and partner acceptance remain unestablished.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Submit business details, check data, resolve gaps, receive score and publish certificate. Corrections or evidence collection may delay value.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More permissioned assessments can improve comparison ranges and spread standards maintenance cost, if consistent methods prevent noisy data.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
