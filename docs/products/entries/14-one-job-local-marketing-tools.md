## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Capable local-business owner payer; assess a $99 review-request tool kit as the first example in this broader product family.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Additional locations, niche packs and useful upgrades can generate repeat purchases; the basic review-request job does not require a monthly subscription.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse the request generator, QR/landing template and instructions; business staff install and operate it without custom implementation by ZedBiz.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned tool IP, niche variants, brand and purchaser list can be transferred; platform dependencies still require maintenance.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator handles releases and bounded support. Jack informs the product specification and exceptions, assumed 0–2 hours/week at steady state.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Owner generates a usable review request and puts it into the customer journey; completing that first useful job creates competence.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A thin or ageing review profile is a trigger, but free review links and platform tools compete with the $99 kit.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts tailored requests and instructions from structured inputs; humans check releases and exceptions, not each routine generated request.
- **Route to the payer:** Website providers and local-business educators could recommend the kit for a referral share; marketplace listing or partner acceptance is not assumed secured.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Buy, enter business details, generate a request, test its link and place it at the intended customer touchpoint. Existing website access is required.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** One supported tool serves more businesses; niche variations reuse the core engine and customers may buy adjacent one-job tools.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
