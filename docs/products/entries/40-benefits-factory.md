# 40. Benefits Factory

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Benefits Factory
- **Idea:** Build one reusable portfolio of tangible benefits that organizations can include with their existing customer, member, employee, franchisee, or subscriber relationship.
- **How:** Negotiate and maintain benefits centrally; package them under channel-owner brands; make membership value visible through real savings or useful access.
- **Buyer:** Agencies, chambers, associations, SaaS companies, franchise systems, employers, credit unions, and other relationship owners.
- **Price:** Model not set; likely B2B licence, per-member economics, vendor funding, or blended model.
- **Tier:** structural / potentially high
- **Shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Type:** business structure
- **Status:** exploration
- **Related ideas:** [39. Collective Purchasing Engine](39-collective-purchasing-engine.md) · [37. Product Ingredient Company (B2B2C layer)](37-product-ingredient-company-b2b2c-layer.md)
- **Price basis:** Unvalidated catalog hypothesis; not evidence of willingness to pay. Sources below establish the concept; no verified market price is claimed.
- **Sources:** Z3 discussion and `briefing/lessons.md` in ZedBiz key-info repo, 2026-09-29.
- **Notes:** Combines Buying Club economics with the Product Ingredient Company. The organization is paying to make its existing relationship more valuable, sticky, and differentiated.
