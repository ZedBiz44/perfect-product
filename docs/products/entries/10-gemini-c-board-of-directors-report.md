## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Local-business owner payer; assess a $197 report for one submitted operating bottleneck, with bounded input and no consultation or implementation upsell.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** A different bottleneck or quarterly review can prompt another report, but one report may solve the immediate need without repeat purchase.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse intake and analysis structure; each buyer's inputs still require processing and substantive human plausibility checks.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned intake, evaluation rules and report system can transfer; confidential client answers are excluded from reusable assets without permission.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A trained analyst signs off reports using a bounded review standard; Jack handles rare product exceptions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The buyer gets a clearer choice and next action, but a report can become another unread document and does not deliver the business outcome itself.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A stuck decision provides a trigger; free AI advice competes and $197 depends on synthesis quality and the usefulness of supplied information.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI analyzes from several perspectives and assembles the report; a human checks each buyer's assumptions and recommendation quality. Per-buyer review is material.
- **Route to the payer:** Bookkeepers or business advisers can refer a fixed report for referral income while retaining advisory relationships; endorsement and access need work.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Complete a structured intake, supply examples, receive report, review assumptions and choose an action. Buyer effort and action remain necessary.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Templates and software spread fixed cost, but per-report analyst review limits the scale benefit and must be priced into margins.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
