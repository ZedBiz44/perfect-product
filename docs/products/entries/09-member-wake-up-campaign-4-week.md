## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Paid-community owner payer; assess the existing four-week campaign at US$129, owner-run and separate from the weekly Shovel format.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** A new member cohort or later quiet period creates another campaign occasion; monthly renewal is not inherent in a four-week pack.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse the Return–Complete–Prove–Continue sequence and assets; owners deliver the campaign and handle members.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned campaign library, implementation examples and customer relationships form transferable assets, subject to contribution rights.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator prepares and supports the pack; owner staff run the room. Jack has occasional product oversight, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The owner sees completed member actions accumulate across four weeks; that scheduled harvest matches the campaign's promised timing.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A quiet paid group creates a trigger, but owner effort and uncertain member participation make US$129 a considered purchase.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts missions, posts and tracking summaries; humans QA the shared campaign. Member facilitation belongs to the buyer.
- **Route to the payer:** Membership consultants can bundle the campaign into community refresh work for margin; convincing them to carry it is still required.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Buy, schedule four weeks, post activities, facilitate responses and review completions. Value builds over weeks and depends on owner execution.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** One campaign serves multiple communities; repeat editions reuse delivery and partner relationships without additional hosting by ZedBiz.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
