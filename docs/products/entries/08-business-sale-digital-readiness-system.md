## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Business-broker firm payer; assess a $997 fixed digital-sale-file licence for repeated seller intake, without valuation or transaction advice.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Additional offices, seller-volume licences and substantive updates can prompt another payment; a one-time licence alone has weak repeat economics.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse inventory forms, ownership prompts and intake templates; sellers and brokers supply records and resolve their own missing documents.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned forms, readiness taxonomy, brand and licence relationships accumulate; private seller data is not presumed transferable business IP.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A product operator maintains forms with subject review; brokers own transactions. Jack supplies occasional commercial perspective, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A seller hands the broker an organized digital-asset inventory instead of scattered passwords and files; a usable intake file signals success.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** New seller onboarding supplies a clear moment, but brokers may already have adequate checklists; the proposed price needs a specific gap.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI organizes submitted inventory and drafts missing-item prompts; humans maintain the shared template while brokers verify each seller's records.
- **Route to the payer:** Broker training networks and succession-adviser newsletters could distribute a sample for referral income; credibility and partner access remain assumptions.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** License, brand the intake, invite seller, collect inventory and review gaps. The seller's record-gathering workload remains substantial.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More broker licences spread maintenance cost; the same intake system can serve more sellers without Jack joining transactions.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
