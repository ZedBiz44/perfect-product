# 4. Door Opener

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Door Opener
- **Idea:** Give an agency one tested, niche-specific free win it can offer a prospect to open a sales conversation.
- **How:** Packaged prospect-facing win (e.g. Google profile improvement for dentists) with evidence of what works; agency runs it; Jack does not take sales work.
- **Buyer:** Marketing agencies / GHL-style agency owners who struggle to get clients.
- **Price:** ~$99–$249 one-time per niche pack, or low monthly refill of new niche wins.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Status:** hopper
- **Sources:** `docs/hopper/door-opener.md`, `docs/perspectives/grok.md`, `docs/ingredients.md`
- **Notes:** Must differ from free audits agencies already use; open research question.
