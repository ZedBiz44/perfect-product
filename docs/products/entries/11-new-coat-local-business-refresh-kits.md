## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency reseller payer; assess a $249 reopening announcement kit using fixed editable layouts, with the end business supplying approved photos and details.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Coat of paint. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** New openings, offers and seasonal refreshes create repeat occasions across an agency's clients; each individual business may buy infrequently.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse layouts, copy patterns and asset sizes; buyer changes approved fields. Bespoke design and repeated revisions are outside the assessed package.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned design system, occasion library and reseller list accumulate, with licensed fonts/images and contributor rights required.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A creative operator packages and checks each edition; resellers serve their clients. Jack handles occasional direction, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The business publishes a coherent refreshed set and sees a before/after improvement; the reveal is the satisfaction event.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A reopening date creates urgency, but $249 competes with Canva templates and an agency's designer; buyers must value the finished occasion package.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts copy and asset variants; human creative QA happens per shared edition. Resellers verify each client's supplied facts and visuals.
- **Route to the payer:** Design educators, agency trainers and chamber service partners could resell for margin; adoption needs a clear occasion-specific demonstration.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose kit, supply approved details/photos, edit fixed fields, check and publish. Value appears after buyer customization, not at download.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More resellers spread design cost and distribute repeat editions; tight scope prevents scale from turning into a custom studio.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
