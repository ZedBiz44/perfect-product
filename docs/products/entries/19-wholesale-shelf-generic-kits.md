## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical killed model: agency pays $297/month for generic seasonal kits licensed across its clients. Assess the generic contents as recorded.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Seasonal campaign deadlines recur, giving the licence a clear replenishment schedule if the agency keeps deploying kits.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One generic edition serves many agencies; they perform client customization and distribution.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** A library and customer list transfer, but commodity contents provide weak appreciation or defensibility.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator manages production and access; agency staff serve clients. Jack handles occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Campaign results could provide a harvest, but generic kits may pile up without being used across varied client needs.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The recorded rejection identifies undifferentiated commodity content; shared operating assumptions do not make $297 an obvious purchase over free alternatives.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts seasonal assets and formats; a human checks one edition. Buyers handle client-specific adaptation.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency content educators could resell for margin, but they already have competing shelves and limited incentive to promote another generic one.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose a kit, adapt to client, configure delivery, launch and follow up; generic content leaves more customization with the agency.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared production and distribution spread fixed costs over more licence holders, even though those economics do not solve weak demand.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
