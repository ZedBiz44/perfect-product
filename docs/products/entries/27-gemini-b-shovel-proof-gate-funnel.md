## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Assess the original free diagnostic funnel with $19 DIY fallback and consulting for qualified prospects. It remains an internal-tool hypothesis with a founder conflict.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** The gate itself is free; DIY follow-ons and consulting can create revenue, but repeat economics depend on the offers behind the gate.
- **Reusable production and costs:** The selected commercial path leads to bespoke consulting, so reusing a quiz does not make the core paid fulfillment build-once.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Assessment logic and permissioned lead records accumulate; the consulting relationship remains tied to personal expertise.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Qualified prospects are routed into Jack-led advisory work, directly conflicting with the recorded no-client-work core constraint.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A prospect gets a clearer diagnosis, but harsh rejection can frustrate buyers and a label alone does not solve the underlying problem.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An immediate bottleneck may prompt a $19 DIY purchase, but artificial exclusion does not by itself establish useful buying value.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI summarizes responses and suggests routes; subsequent consulting still relies on human judgment per client.
- **Route to the payer:** Partner educators can share the free diagnostic for useful triage or referral income, but a funnel does not generate its own traffic.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Answer the assessment, review the result, choose a follow-on and act. Benefits depend on a second step after the diagnostic.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** The gate handles more submissions cheaply, but growth in qualified consulting work creates additional human delivery rather than strong product leverage.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
