# 36. Recurring Customer-Value Kits (weekly OS)

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Recurring Customer-Value Kits / Weekly Operating System
- **Idea:** "This week is handled" — completed weekly campaign-in-a-box that removes the recurring obligation, not "content."
- **How:** Broader than Skool: memberships, coaches, associations, franchises, SaaS CS, agencies, publishers, chambers; white-label OK.
- **Buyer:** Any operator with a weekly customer-value obligation + distributors who own audiences.
- **Price:** Founding pilot ~$29–$49 for first month (Z3).
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Status:** mashup
- **Sources:** `docs/perspectives/z3.md`, `docs/concepts/shovel-your-biz-shit.md`
- **Notes:** Mashup/reframing of Shovel as operating dependency + distributor thesis.
