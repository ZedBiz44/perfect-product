## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical killed model: agency pays $8/client/month for a monthly narrative and usable asset. The criticism that this sells agencies their own job remains.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Client reporting recurs monthly, supplying a recurring payment occasion while the agency retains its client base.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Templates are shared, but mapping each client's data, correcting attribution and checking narratives create per-client work at a low price.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Templates and agency accounts accumulate; client-owned data and dependence on agency relationships limit what transfers.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator handles accounts and exceptions; agencies approve their clients' narratives. Jack is not the monthly account manager, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The agency gets its monthly reporting task partly handled, but the client may see another generic report rather than useful new value.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The recorded model asks agencies to buy their existing reporting job again without a distinct benefit; the shared assumptions do not repair that objection.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts from exports, while people resolve and check individual-client facts every month. That routine review grows with client count.
- **Route to the payer:** Reporting-tool educators could refer for a share, but the overlap with existing reports gives distributors a weak reason to carry it.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Connect data, map accounts, generate, verify and send; value follows reliable data preparation and repeated agency review.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared templates lower drafting cost, while customer-data exceptions and low per-client prices constrain margin improvements with volume.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
