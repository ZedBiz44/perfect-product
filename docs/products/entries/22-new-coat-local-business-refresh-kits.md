# 22. New Coat local-business refresh kits

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** New Coat refresh kits
- **Idea:** Fixed "new coat" local-business refresh packages at life moments (new location, new offer, post-renovation, slow-season reset).
- **How:** One finished photographable package; sold to agencies/chambers who resell; GHL only delivers assets; no custom design creep.
- **Buyer:** Agencies, chambers, franchise HQs (channel); end local businesses.
- **Price:** ~$149–$499 per kit to channel; marked up to end client.
- **Tier:** mid–high
- **Shape:** Coat of paint
- **Status:** hopper
