## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Assess the original Jack-owned paid AI-mentor community at $49/month for marketing operators. Do not silently replace it with product 29's B2B licence.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Members pay for continuing answers, resources and interaction, providing a recurring membership mechanism if they keep participating.
- **Reusable production and costs:** AI response infrastructure is reusable, but moderation, safety, support and social coordination increase with member activity.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Knowledge assets and membership relationships accumulate, but community culture and Jack's reputation make transfer less reliable.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Jack still owns the room and is the strategic authority; assigning AI mentors or a VA does not resolve the recorded founder-host failure mode.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A useful answer can feel supportive, but AI replies alone may not deliver real peer belonging or sustained community culture.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An operator with a live business question has a trigger, but free AI and established communities compete with the $49 membership.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI answers and routes routine questions; humans moderate conflict and maintain culture. The social work remains a substantial part of fulfillment.
- **Route to the payer:** Partner newsletters could refer members for revenue share; attracting and retaining a trusted community still needs ongoing promotion and social effort.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Join, orient, post a question, assess the response and participate. Value depends on answer quality and active members rather than access alone.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More useful discussions can strengthen the knowledge base, but moderation and founder expectations can rise with membership.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
