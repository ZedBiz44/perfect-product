# 39. Collective Purchasing Engine

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Collective Purchasing Engine
- **Idea:** Aggregate thousands of SMB buyers first, then use their collective purchasing demand to decide what ZedBiz should negotiate, white-label, license, manufacture, or acquire.
- **How:** Distribute through agencies and other channel owners; measure repeated spend categories; negotiate or create better offers as purchasing volume grows.
- **Buyer:** Agencies, associations, chambers, franchise systems, and their SMB client/member bases.
- **Price:** Model not set; possible vendor commissions, member economics, agency licensing, or shared savings.
- **Tier:** structural / potentially high
- **Shape:** Tool in the hand / Harvest Gala depending on benefit delivered
- **Type:** business structure
- **Status:** exploration
- **Related ideas:** [40. Benefits Factory](40-benefits-factory.md) · [41. Micro-product Holding Company](41-micro-product-holding-company.md)
- **Price basis:** Unvalidated catalog hypothesis; not evidence of willingness to pay. Sources below establish the concept; no verified market price is claimed.
- **Sources:** Z3 discussion and `briefing/lessons.md` in ZedBiz key-info repo, 2026-09-29.
- **Notes:** Extends Jack's existing Brand Ambassador / "Costco for Business" concept. Key distinction: aggregate demand before deciding which products to build or source. More participants can directly improve purchasing power (Newton's Rule).
