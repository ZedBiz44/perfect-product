## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical rejected model: agency-operator subscriber pays $12/month for Jack's practitioner letter and reusable artifact. Do not remove its author dependence.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Subscribers pay for a fresh practical perspective and artifact each period; the continuing editorial promise is the renewal reason.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One issue reaches many readers, although research and distribution still consume recurring effort.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** The archive and list accumulate, but readers attracted to Jack's personal judgment make transfer to a different owner less reliable.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Jack's practitioner voice is the product's central promise; routine delegation changes that promise. Regular authorship conflicts with founder independence.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A useful idea can satisfy the weekly appetite, but unread newsletters and free commentary make consistent consumption uncertain.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An operator seeking useful perspective may buy, but there is no strong universal purchase-now trigger and abundant free alternatives compete.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI gathers sources and drafts; Jack provides the core lived judgment and final editorial position for each issue.
- **Route to the payer:** Newsletter exchanges and relevant educators can refer readers for a share; audience trust and attention must still be earned through publishing.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Subscribe, read one short note, select the artifact and apply it. The first useful insight can arrive with the next issue.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Delivery cost per reader falls, but founder editorial time and dependence on his reputation limit operating leverage.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
