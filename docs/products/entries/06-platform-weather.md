## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Marketing-agency payer; assess a $99/month alert and client-communication subscription covering a bounded set of platforms.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** New outages and policy changes continually recreate the monitoring and client-communication job, giving subscribers a reason to retain coverage.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One verified alert serves many agencies, but reliable monitoring, false alarms, platform breadth and editorial availability create significant ongoing costs.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** An owned incident archive, taxonomy, communication templates and subscriber relationships accumulate; source rights and data provenance must be maintained.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A monitoring operator verifies incidents and sends notices using an escalation rota. Jack handles occasional policy decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** An agency identifies an incident and sends a clear client message promptly; the usable message and avoided confusion provide immediate competence.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An active outage makes the need obvious, but free status pages and peer groups compete; $99 needs useful synthesis beyond repeating notices.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI collects signals, clusters incidents and drafts notices; humans verify one incident-level bulletin and exceptions rather than every customer's message.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency newsletters and operations communities can carry a useful public alert and paid referral link; credibility and placement need to be earned.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Subscribe, choose platforms, receive a relevant alert, check applicability and adapt the client message. Value arrives when a covered incident occurs.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** The same verified incident serves more agencies at lower per-subscriber cost; properly permissioned reports can improve detection coverage.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
