## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Illustrative first created brand: AcreageReady, licensing product 07's regional kit to a rural brokerage at $997 plus optional updates. No brand has been launched.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** More brokerage licences and regional updates can repeat revenue; each brand needs its own renewal reason and cannot borrow success from the portfolio label.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse one regional product, but new brands and jurisdictions require research, rights and specialist review.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Separate owned brand, IP, customer contracts and operating records can form a sellable asset independent of the wider ZedBiz business.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A brand operator runs fulfilment; Jack still directs opportunity selection and brand decisions, assumed 2–4 hours/week per active new brand.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** In the example, the buyer uses the rural-property checklist before an offer and asks a better question; other brands need their own experience design.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A brokerage's next rural buyer intake creates a trigger, but its existing checklist and unproven licence value remain barriers.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts the shared product and marketing assets; humans review regional content per edition. Broker staff advise individual buyers.
- **Route to the payer:** Rural brokerage trainers and lending partners could introduce the brand for referral value; Jack's agriculture background is context, not guaranteed access.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Broker brands and deploys the kit, buyer gathers information and consults professionals. Each new brand may have a different adoption burden.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared production systems help across brands, but fragmented audiences and compliance/maintenance needs can offset portfolio economies.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
