## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer facing a cancellation conversation; assess a $99/month self-serve proof-packet tool using its own client exports. Jack's objections remain.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Monthly access supports repeated client review and cancellation events; ongoing value depends on having enough client accounts to use it regularly.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse the packet engine, but inconsistent exports, account mapping and disputed metrics add per-account support and QA costs.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned packet software, templates and permissioned outcome records can transfer; clients retain rights to their underlying data.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A product operator handles access and incidents; the agency signs off each packet. Jack has occasional product oversight, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The agency produces a clear answer to 'what did you do?' before a client meeting; a usable accurate packet is the immediate success signal.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A cancellation threat makes the need urgent, but built-in reports and AI summaries compete; a packet cannot rescue poor underlying results.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI assembles and explains supplied metrics; agency humans must verify each client's attribution and claims. Per-client verification remains material.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency operations educators could demonstrate packets and refer users for a share; data-access concerns and competing reporting tools complicate adoption.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Connect or upload, map metrics, generate, verify and present. Value arrives at the next review, after meaningful data preparation.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared software spreads fixed costs, but customer-specific data failures and integrations can make support grow alongside adoption.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
