## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Existing Shovel owner subscriber pays an additional $15/month for themed-day assets. Assess as an optional layer on product 01.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Weekly themed slots recur, so the add-on supplies fresh scaffolding alongside the base subscription.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Shared themes and assets are produced once per edition; customers handle their own posting and member replies.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned theme library, bundle rights and subscription relationships accumulate; the add-on is transferable with or licensed to the host product.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator delivers the bundle; Jack's weekly selection remains shared with the core edition, assumed within the same 1–3 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The owner fills additional posting slots, but more posts may create noise instead of useful member actions.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An owner wanting a fuller week has a trigger, but the extra $15 competes with free prompts and may add unnecessary content.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts themed prompts and visual variants; human QA occurs once per shared edition, without handling each owner's members.
- **Route to the payer:** Offer a preview inside the base product's checkout and weekly delivery; the existing host earns extra revenue. This assumes product 01 first wins buyers.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Preview, add to subscription, receive the bundle, select relevant days and post. The owner can use it in the same week's schedule.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared theme production costs spread across add-on subscribers, with low incremental delivery cost if editions stay reusable.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
