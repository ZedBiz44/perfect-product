## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer adds a $197/month campaign licence to its existing client service, using one weekly retention asset and seasonal campaign editions.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Agencies have recurring client-value and seasonal selling obligations, creating repeated use occasions for new editions.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse each edition across agencies; the agency customizes and executes for clients without ZedBiz managing accounts.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned campaign assets, edition history and agency licence relationships accumulate and transfer with documented rights.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A campaign operator publishes and supports editions; Jack handles occasional strategy, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Agencies can show a season's useful client activity, but combining weekly drops with bigger campaigns risks an unfocused pile of assets.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An upcoming client campaign provides a trigger; the $197 licence must beat internal content and existing tool libraries.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts and formats editions; shared human QA checks offers and claims. Client-specific execution belongs to the purchaser.
- **Route to the payer:** Offer as an add-on through an existing campaign-product customer list or reseller checkout; conditional on that host already having paying agencies.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Pick edition, align it with client offer, customize, deploy and follow up. The buyer still performs campaign management.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Recurring editions spread production costs over licences and use the same agency relationships for later seasonal sales.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
