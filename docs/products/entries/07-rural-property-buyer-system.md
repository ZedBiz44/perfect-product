## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Rural real-estate brokerage payer; assess a single-region pre-offer kit at $997 plus an optional $97/month update licence. Property buyers use the kit.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** New buyer cohorts support repeat use, but renewal requires substantive regional updates or additional licensed offices; a static checklist is reusable without renewal.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One regional kit serves many brokerages, but expert review, regional variations and customer setup reduce pure build-once leverage.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned regional kit IP, channel contracts and maintained question sets can transfer. Jack's agriculture background informs questions, not professional certification.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator maintains editions with a qualified regional reviewer; broker staff handle property-specific advice. Jack is occasional editor, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** At a viewing, a buyer identifies an overlooked question about the property and asks the right professional before offering; the brokerage supplies that competence.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A new rural buyer intake is a trigger; $997 must beat the brokerage's existing checklist and cannot promise prevention of financial loss.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts checklists, assembles regional source changes and packages follow-up assets; humans verify each edition. Broker staff handle individual properties.
- **Route to the payer:** Brokerage trainers or land-lending networks could introduce the licence for member value or referral fees; relationships and access are unconfirmed.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Broker brands the fixed kit, sends it at intake, buyer completes the visit scorecard and asks professionals. Useful value requires an actual property visit.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More licensees spread regional review costs, but new jurisdictions add review and maintenance rather than free scale.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
