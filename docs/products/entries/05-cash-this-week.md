## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer with a client's permitted dormant list; assess a $149 fixed reactivation pack, retaining the recorded objections about generic offers.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Different seasonal offers or niches create repeat occasions, but a buyer can reuse the same messages without repurchasing.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse campaign copy, workflow pattern and checklist; the agency loads its own contacts, checks consent and runs the campaign.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned campaign editions, brand and buyer list transfer, assuming rights to any contributed materials and permissioned examples.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator maintains packs and answers bounded product questions. Jack handles exceptions rather than campaigns, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Booked responses during the campaign create a harvest feeling; the pack alone cannot promise cash in a week or overcome a weak list.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A slow booking week is a trigger, but existing GoHighLevel reactivation workflows and free copy undermine urgency to buy this pack.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts niche messages, variants and instructions; a human checks the shared sequence. Buyers approve and send their own campaigns.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency trainers can recommend a niche demonstration with an affiliate share; earning placement requires a clear advantage over their existing templates.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose the offer, clean the permitted list, load messages, test and launch. Buyer setup and actual replies determine when value arrives.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Each added buyer shares fixed pack costs; seasonal variants reuse the same delivery and distribution systems.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
