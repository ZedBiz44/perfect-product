## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Course creator pays $197/month for a white-label action layer on one repeatable marketing course; learners use it without ZedBiz coaching them.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Each cohort needs implementation prompts and updated tasks, giving the creator a recurring licence reason tied to course delivery.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse action patterns, but course mapping and keeping tasks aligned with changing lessons create meaningful setup and maintenance.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned action templates, mapping system and course-provider contracts accumulate; creators retain their lesson rights.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator manages mappings and support; course staff handle students. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A learner completes the first concrete course task, while the payer sees learners using what was sold; the completed task is the success signal.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A creator facing low implementation has a trigger, but $197 depends on the layer improving use beyond existing worksheets and reminders.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts tasks and adapts reminders from approved lesson maps; humans verify shared mappings and exceptions. No individual accountability coaching is included.
- **Route to the payer:** Course-platform implementers could include the layer for reseller margin; course access, mapping effort and partner endorsement need resolution.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Creator supplies outline, reviews the task map, publishes it, invites learners and checks completion. Initial mapping is a real project.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Reusable task patterns improve production efficiency, but different course content can cause per-creator maintenance to grow.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
