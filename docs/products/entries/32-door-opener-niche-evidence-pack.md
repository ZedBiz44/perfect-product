## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer buys a $199 dental-niche opening pack plus sourced demonstrations. Evidence examples must be permissioned or public and honestly labelled.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Another niche or a useful evidence refresh can trigger another purchase; the initial pack may remain usable for a long period.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reusable scripts scale, but finding, licensing and checking credible niche examples costs more than generating generic outreach copy.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** An owned method, rights-cleared evidence library and buyer list accumulate as transferable assets; invented case studies have no value.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A research editor maintains the pack and an operator supports it. Jack handles occasional standards decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The agency can show a relevant example and perform a small useful improvement, making its first prospect conversation more concrete.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** Client acquisition pain creates a trigger, but $199 must beat free audits and credible case studies may not transfer to the prospect's situation.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI finds candidate sources and drafts scripts; humans check evidence quality and permitted reuse for every new case added to the shared pack.
- **Route to the payer:** Niche agency trainers can demonstrate the pack and earn margin; they will need credible examples and a reason to trust the claims.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Select niche, inspect example limits, adapt the free win, obtain necessary access and approach prospects. The agency still performs the selling.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Each verified example can serve many pack buyers; a broader rights-cleared library supports relevant cross-sales through the same channels.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
