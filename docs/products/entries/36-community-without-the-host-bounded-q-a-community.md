## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical archived model: agency-operator member pays $39/month for a room with Jack's weekly Q&A. Optional advisory remains part of the recorded ladder.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Belonging without hosting. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Members pay for continuing access, answers and peer interaction, providing a recurring membership mechanism.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Playbooks and sessions can reach many members, but moderation, Q&A demand and advisory work grow with the room.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** The archive and membership list accumulate, but the promise of Jack's presence makes transfer to another operator difficult.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Weekly Jack Q&A and advisory remain core promised value. VA logistics do not remove his hosting obligation or repair the failed social-engine approach.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Members may get a useful answer and connection, but belonging depends on active peers and reliable human participation.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A practitioner with a live problem may pay $39, but free groups and AI compete; Jack's presence is also the very dependency the framework rejects.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI summarizes, drafts resources and answers routine questions; humans still deliver live Q&A and sustain social culture.
- **Route to the payer:** Practitioner newsletters and affiliates can refer for a share, but Jack-led content and appearances may still be needed to earn trust.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Join, orient, attend at the scheduled time, ask and apply the answer. Waiting and participation remain necessary to get value.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More members can create useful peer exchanges, but moderation and founder demand can grow alongside membership.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
