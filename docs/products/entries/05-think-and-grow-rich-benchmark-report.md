# 5. Think and Grow Rich (benchmark report)

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Think and Grow Rich / benchmark report
- **Idea:** Pull together what actually works across businesses into a useful outcome-evidence report that gives owners a reason to stay.
- **How:** Aggregate outcome evidence into a recurring or annual report; stickiness ingredient for another product, not the main offer.
- **Buyer:** Agency owners / community owners who want proof of what works; later, as upgrade for Shovel or Trophy products.
- **Price:** Included in higher tier, or ~$49–$99/report, or annual ~$199.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Trophy (status/data) / Creates Gold ingredient
- **Status:** hopper
- **Sources:** `docs/hopper/think-and-grow-rich.md`, `docs/perspectives/grok.md`
- **Notes:** No proprietary campaign-results dataset in repo yet.
