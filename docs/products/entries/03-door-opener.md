# 3. Door Opener

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Door Opener
- **Idea:** Give an agency one tested, niche-specific free win it can offer a prospect to open a sales conversation.
- **How:** Packaged prospect-facing win (e.g. Google profile improvement for dentists) with evidence of what works; agency runs it; Jack does not take sales work.
- **Buyer:** Marketing agencies / GHL-style agency owners who struggle to get clients.
- **Price:** ~$99–$249 one-time per niche pack, or low monthly refill of new niche wins.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Type:** product
- **Status:** hopper
- **Related ideas:** [32. Door Opener + niche evidence pack](32-door-opener-niche-evidence-pack.md) · [22. Think and Grow Rich (benchmark report)](22-think-and-grow-rich-benchmark-report.md)
- **Price basis:** Unvalidated catalog hypothesis; not evidence of willingness to pay. Sources below establish the concept; no verified market price is claimed.
- **Sources:** `docs/hopper/door-opener.md`, `docs/perspectives/grok.md`, `docs/ingredients.md`
- **Notes:** Must differ from free audits agencies already use; open research question.

## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer running its own prospect outreach; assess one dental-practice profile-improvement pack at $149, inside the recorded range.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Agencies may buy another niche pack or update, but the same niche's opening move can be reused without another payment.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One niche demonstration, checklist and outreach script are sold repeatedly; the agency executes prospect work and supplies its own account access.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned niche playbooks, permissioned demonstrations and a buyer list accumulate; the business sells reusable IP rather than Jack's sales time.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator updates and checks each pack; agency staff do sales. Jack handles occasional product decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week at steady state.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The agency completes a small visible profile improvement and has a useful opening conversation; the first completed improvement shows competence.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A weak prospect pipeline is a trigger, but free audits and agency experience make the $149 advantage harder to explain.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts niche research, scripts and improvement suggestions; a human validates the shared method. The customer checks each prospect's actual details.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency trainers and niche newsletters can demonstrate the free win for affiliate income; an operator must earn placement and explain differentiation.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Select a niche, inspect a prospect, obtain permission where needed, apply the suggested improvement and use the script. Sales effort remains with buyer.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More agencies share the cost of maintaining a niche pack; a larger pack library supports cross-sales through the same channel.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
