# 13. Client Action Layer

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Client Action Layer
- **Idea:** Productize the gap between buying and using — behavior-change support so clients actually do the thing.
- **How:** Implementation/action packs bolted onto courses, retainers, or software; risk of becoming service work.
- **Buyer:** Course creators, coaches, SaaS, agencies whose clients don't implement.
- **Price:** Licence ~$97–$497/mo white-label, or $29–$99 end-user add-on.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Tool in the hand
- **Type:** add-on
- **Status:** hopper
- **Related ideas:** [37. Product Ingredient Company (B2B2C layer)](37-product-ingredient-company-b2b2c-layer.md)
- **Price basis:** Unvalidated catalog hypothesis; not evidence of willingness to pay. Sources below establish the concept; no verified market price is claimed.

- **Notes:** Related to Z3 Product Ingredient Company / CourseFinish illustrative brand.
