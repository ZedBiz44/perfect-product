# 7. Sponsor layer on weekly item

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Sponsor layer
- **Idea:** A tool company or supplier pays so audience owners can share a useful weekly item (item stays useful without the logo).
- **How:** Sponsor funds distribution of an already-useful weekly unit; agency/owner keeps client relationship; voluntary next step only.
- **Buyer:** Tool vendors / suppliers as payers; owners as distributors of free-to-them value.
- **Price:** Sponsor pays ~$500–$5,000/mo per niche (wild guess); owners may get free.
- **Tier:** mid–high (sponsor side)
- **Shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire (for end users)
- **Type:** funding layer
- **Status:** hopper
- **Related ideas:** [34. Shovel + Sponsor layer](34-shovel-sponsor-layer.md) · [1. Shovel Your Biz-Shit](01-shovel-your-biz-shit.md)
- **Price basis:** Unvalidated catalog hypothesis; not evidence of willingness to pay. Sources below establish the concept; no verified market price is claimed.
- **Sources:** `docs/hopper/sponsor-layer.md`, `docs/perspectives/manus.md`, `docs/perspectives/grok.md`
- **Notes:** Larger coordinated sponsor-funded "box" failed cold-beer test; keep as later funding layer.
