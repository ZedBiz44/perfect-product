## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Illustrative first shelf item: a $49 review-request kit for capable local operators, drawn from product 14. This is an assessment scenario, not a chosen launch.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Adjacent job kits and additional locations can produce repeat purchases; a single small kit has no inherent recurring payment.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One finished kit and self-serve checkout serve many buyers; custom installation and consulting are outside this scenario.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned kit IP, catalog, brand and transaction relationships accumulate into a transferable product line.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A catalog operator maintains products and support; Jack selects occasional additions, assumed 0–2 hours/week once the first kit is stable.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The operator finishes a review-request setup and can immediately use it; the shelf earns value through completing a named job.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A sparse review profile provides a trigger, but a $49 kit still competes with free instructions and native tools.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI generates structured request variants and prepares shared kit assets; humans check releases and exceptions rather than each routine use.
- **Route to the payer:** Website providers can refer the kit for a share and job-specific search can attract buyers; neither partner distribution nor search ranking is established.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose the named job, buy, enter business details, generate and test the request. The scenario assumes ordinary website access and capability.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Later customers spread kit cost and adjacent products reuse checkout, support patterns and buyer relationships; avoid stockpiling unwanted inventory.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
