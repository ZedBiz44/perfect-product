## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Agency payer serving seasonal home-service businesses; assess a $399 spring-opening campaign edition, run by the agency for its own clients.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Harvest Gala. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Distinct seasonal buying windows recur annually, creating a concrete reason to buy refreshed offers and later-season editions.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One niche/season pack serves multiple agencies; buyer handles list, budget, local facts and campaign operation.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned seasonal IP, permissioned examples and agency relationships accumulate across annual editions and can transfer.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Campaign operator maintains the seasonal edition; agencies run client campaigns. Jack contributes occasional positioning input, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A visible collection of seasonal enquiries or bookings creates the harvest; timing matches the buyer's campaign window rather than instant income.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The approaching busy season creates urgency, but $399 needs a useful niche-specific offer and competes with existing agency assets.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts niche messages, landing-copy variants and follow-up assets; human QA checks one shared edition, while agencies approve local details.
- **Route to the payer:** Home-service agency trainers and supplier marketing programmes could distribute for margin or member value; permission and placement remain proposed.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Select season, adapt local offer, prepare list/ads, launch and follow up. Results depend on the buyer's execution and local market.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Seasonal research and production costs spread over agencies; later seasons reuse the distribution and production system.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
