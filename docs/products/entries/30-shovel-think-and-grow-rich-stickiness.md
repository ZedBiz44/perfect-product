## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Shovel owner payer adds $20/month for periodic outcome comparisons. Assess one shared weekly edition plus permissioned benchmark reports.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Weekly actions plus periodic learning from outcomes create replenishment and review occasions for the combined subscription.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse content and reports, but consent, data collection and normalization add costs beyond the base weekly pack.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned content plus rights-cleared outcome records form a more substantial transferable library and analytical asset.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator handles collection and delivery; Jack calibrates content and interpretation at shared-edition level, assumed 1–3 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Owner publishes a useful action, then sees how its result compares with relevant peers; weekly relief is reinforced by feedback.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A renewal or planning moment can justify the extra $20, but comparisons must be relevant enough to beat informal peer discussion.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts content and aggregates results; human judgment checks comparable cohorts and interpretation. Data review can grow with participation diversity.
- **Route to the payer:** Offer a sample comparison and upgrade inside the base product's delivery and checkout; this relies on a host with paying, participating owners.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Use the weekly unit, record agreed outcomes, submit permissioned data and read a comparison. Reporting adds effort beyond posting alone.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More comparable records can improve relevance while report costs spread across subscribers; poor-quality data would weaken this mechanism.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
