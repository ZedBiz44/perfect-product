## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Assess a local-business association as one example payer at $39/month for a finished weekly member-value kit; broader markets remain open.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** The association has a fresh member-value obligation each week, creating a replenishment reason beyond a static library.
- **Reusable production and costs:** One industry edition serves many associations; staff choose and brand assets without commissioning a custom campaign.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned editions, operator documentation and association relationships accumulate as transferable product assets.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator packages and delivers; Jack still calibrates practical usefulness once per shared week, assumed 1–3 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Association staff can say 'this week is handled' after publishing a useful member action; actual member use supplies the follow-through signal.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The next member communication deadline supplies a trigger, but existing staff content and free AI compete with the $39 licence.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI researches, drafts and formats one shared edition; humans select and verify that edition. Buyers own member relationships.
- **Route to the payer:** Association-management firms could include a sample in client services for referral income; recruitment and relevance across member groups need work.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Subscribe, choose an edition, add branding, schedule and share. Value begins with completing the weekly communication task.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Each new association shares the same edition cost; reuse across similar organizations improves unit economics without adding hosting.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
