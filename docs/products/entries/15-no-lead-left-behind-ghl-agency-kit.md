## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical rejected offer: GoHighLevel agency payer buys the existing $249 lead-response kit plus optional $99/year updates. Rejection remains in force.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Useful compatibility updates and additional kits can renew revenue; buyers may keep using the initial snapshot without renewing.
- **Reusable production and costs:** The snapshot, install video and checklist are reusable; agencies install and adapt their own accounts within bounded support.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned workflow package, documentation and customer list can transfer, subject to platform and content rights.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** A product operator handles updates and support; agencies own client delivery. Jack is occasional overseer, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A test lead receives the expected response and routes correctly, giving the buyer a concrete first-use success.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** Missed leads provide a clear problem, but established snapshots and native workflows compete; the recorded buyer objection still blocks revival.
- **AI and human work per unit:** Automation routes leads; AI can draft response variations and support explanations. Rule-based workflow execution alone does not establish AI as the core worker.
- **Route to the payer:** Agency educators could resell for margin, but crowding and differentiation make channel access a hurdle; no new partnership is assumed.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Import, configure credentials and routing, review messages, test and activate. Technical account setup is a real buyer burden.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Reusable releases spread maintenance cost over agencies, provided support stays bounded and accounts use a supported configuration.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
