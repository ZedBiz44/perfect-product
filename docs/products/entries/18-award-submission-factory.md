## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Historical killed model: agency payer considers a $299 quarterly award packet for a specific chosen award. Award demand remains unestablished.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Trophy. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Award seasons and additional clients create occasional repeat demand, not an inherent monthly need.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Reuse structure and drafting, but evidence assembly, award-specific rules and client permissions require meaningful work per submission.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Templates and permissioned case-study assets transfer; the business does not automatically own client results or award credibility.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An editor manages submissions and source checks; Jack handles occasional standards decisions, assumed 0–2 hours/week.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** A polished submission feels ready, but the desired trophy depends on an external judge; the purchased packet cannot guarantee recognition.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The recorded offer lacks a supported reason agencies urgently need awards; selecting an award for illustration does not establish a compelling buying case.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI structures and drafts evidence; a human checks each client's facts, permissions and award requirements before delivery.
- **Route to the payer:** Award calendars and agency educators provide a possible route, but organizers may restrict promotion and buyer interest remains weak.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose an award, collect evidence, supply permissions, review draft and submit. Buyer effort and waiting for judging are substantial.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared templates save time, but different award rules and bespoke evidence limit scale benefits.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
