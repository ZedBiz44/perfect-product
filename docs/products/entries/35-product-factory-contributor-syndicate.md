## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Internal production structure, assessed through an illustrative $99 review-request kit sold to local operators via partners. Product economics belong to that example, not a factory subscription.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Tool in the hand. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Repeat revenue comes from additional job products and updates sold by the output business; the internal factory itself has no external payer.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Research and production patterns repeat, but each new product still needs discovery, testing and distribution work; manufacturing digital files is not demand.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Assigned contributor rights, owned product IP and documented production systems can accumulate and transfer with successful products.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operators run research and production, but Jack still selects opportunities and resolves product trade-offs, assumed 2–4 hours/week per active product lane.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The example buyer completes a review-request job; the factory has no direct satisfaction independent of the quality of its output products.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** The example kit addresses a recognizable job at $99, but that buying logic cannot be generalized to all future factory outputs.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI performs research synthesis, drafting and packaging; humans check shared outputs and product decisions, with no routine per-buyer manufacturing role.
- **Route to the payer:** The example uses website-provider referrals for a share; the factory must establish a channel for each product rather than assuming one universal audience.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** The example buyer follows a bounded kit journey; other outputs may need different setup, so the structure has only conditional ease of value.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** Shared tooling and reusable production knowledge help, but new niches and products also multiply coordination and maintenance costs.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
