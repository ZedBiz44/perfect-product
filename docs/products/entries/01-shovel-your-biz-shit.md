# 1. Shovel Your Biz-Shit

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Shovel Your Biz-Shit (weekly useful item for audience owners)
- **Idea:** Give an existing audience owner one small, fresh, useful idea each week they can share with their people under their own name.
- **How:** Weekly drop = one concept + 2–4 engagement posts (including comment-to-get) + small supplementary pack; niche editions of the same format; Jack/AI produce, owner posts.
- **Buyer:** Small-to-mid paid Skool / membership / community owners; local-business coaching groups; optionally large free FB AI group owners (secondary).
- **Price:** ~$29–$49/mo founding; niche editions same format.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire
- **Status:** core candidate
- **Sources:** `docs/concepts/shovel-your-biz-shit.md`, `docs/perspectives/manus.md`, `docs/current-direction.md`
- **Notes:** Formats, vault, and prices unapproved; willingness to pay unproven.
