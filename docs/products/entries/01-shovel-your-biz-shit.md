## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Paid community owner; assess the existing weekly shared drop at $27/month as a working price, retaining the other recorded price hypotheses.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Six Pack of Beer Desire. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** Fresh weekly member activities replace the owner's recurring job of finding something useful to share; renewal depends on continued use.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Produce one edition per niche, deliver it unchanged to many owners, and limit support to access and use; no individual content calendars.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned editions, subscriber relationships, brand and documented production accumulate and can transfer if contributor rights are assigned.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** An operator schedules and delivers; Jack still selects the weekly idea and reviews one shared edition. Assume 1–3 hours/week, not per buyer.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** The owner feels relief when this week's useful post is ready; a member completing its action is the success signal. Content can still sit unused.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** A quiet room before the next posting day creates a trigger, but $27 competes with free AI and the owner's own ideas; retention value is hypothetical.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI researches candidates, drafts posts and packages files; a human selects and checks once per shared edition. Routine work does not multiply per owner.
- **Route to the payer:** Community consultants could offer a sample to owner clients for a referral share; an operator recruits partners. Permission, incentive and reach need work.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Buy, receive the drop, select the relevant post, make a short voice edit and publish. Value begins that week; member response is not guaranteed.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** More owners spread the same edition and review cost across more subscriptions; niche fragmentation and support must stay bounded.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
