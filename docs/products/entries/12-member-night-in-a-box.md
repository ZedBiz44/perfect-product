## Working concept for assessment

Version: WC1 · 2026-09-30 · Cody · Proposed assumptions for scoring, authorized by Jack; not a launch decision.

**Working scenario:** Membership or chamber owner payer; assess a $99 event pack with staff executing the night and no Jack appearance.

**Primary satisfaction shape:** Event Excitement. This is the working interpretation for this scenario; the original Shape field above is retained.

All bullets below are working assumptions or inferences, not observed operating results. The original description and status above remain the recorded history. [Context and assumption basis](../assessment-context.md).

- **Repeat payment:** A calendar of events supports repeat purchases and seasonal bundles, but a successful format can also be reused without paying again.
- **Reusable production and costs:** Theme, mission, invitations and run-of-show are shared assets; venue, host and attendee logistics stay with the purchaser.
- **What accumulates and transfers:** Owned event formats, creative assets and customer relationships accumulate; a trained team can transfer the product line.
- **Operator and Jack’s role:** Operator maintains packs; the customer's staff host. Jack has occasional product input, assumed 0–2 hours/week and no event duty.
- **Satisfaction and success signal:** Participants complete the shared activity and capture the event moment; anticipation and the event peak supply the promised satisfaction.
- **Buying trigger, price and alternatives:** An empty event-calendar slot is a trigger, but free event ideas compete and the owner still bears attendance risk.
- **AI and human work per unit:** AI drafts invitations, prompts and activity variations; a human tests the shared run-of-show. Human hosting is the customer's existing role.
- **Route to the payer:** Chamber service newsletters or community consultants could distribute preview packs for margin; access and their appetite for event products need confirmation.
- **Purchase-to-value journey:** Choose date, invite people, prepare materials, host the activity and share the result. Buyer logistics are meaningful but visible before purchase.
- **Advantage from greater scale:** The same tested night serves more organizations; a larger event catalog supports repeat sales through the same partner channels.

**Unresolved decision:** The payer, price, scope and channel above are a proposed scenario for evaluation. Confirm or change them before any launch; no supplier, partner or customer commitment is implied.
