# 27. Peer-circle operating system

[Index](../README.md) · [Rubric](../../framework/perfect-product-rubric.md)

- **Name:** Peer-circle OS (belonging without hosting)
- **Idea:** Licence prompts, scoreboard, and missions to group owners; their staff/VA runs the room; Jack sells the system.
- **How:** B2B2C structure; owner owns community; avoids Jack-as-mayor failure mode from Community Without the Host.
- **Buyer:** Group owners who want peer energy without inventing it.
- **Price:** ~$49–$199/mo seat licence per community.
- **Tier:** mid
- **Shape:** Belonging without hosting
- **Status:** hopper / mashup
- **Sources:** `docs/framework/satisfaction-shapes.md`, `docs/archive/community-without-the-host.md`
- **Notes:** Mashup: satisfaction shape example + archived community lessons.
